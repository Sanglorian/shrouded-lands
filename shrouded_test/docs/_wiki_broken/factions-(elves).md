The elves of the Court ([Vo Tume](/wiki/vo-tume/) in their own language) are all bound by the same fey contracts, and all ultimately answer to the same [Bloodied King](/wiki/bloodied-king/). The King is named Tuma Yedaard plus a hundred other titles and names dependent upon the situation. Tuma Yedaard is also their name for [the forest](/wiki/the-kingswood/) itself.

Elves are flighty creatures, and their whims change with seasons and sometimes day by day, and sometimes those whims put one elf at odds with another. However, on any given sunny day, an elf will fall into one of four factions.
