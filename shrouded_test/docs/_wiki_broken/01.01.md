[01.01](/wiki/01-01/)

Among the mountains here can be found the enormous corpse of a roc chick, its smell is ripe and strong but even the vultures stay away. As everyone knows, rocs lay their eggs in the air and their chicks must learn to fly before they hit the ground. This one didn't.

# Connections
- Anything said around the corpse of a roc will be repeated by the mockingbird of the Kingswood ([25.07](/wiki/25-07/)).

# Hooks
- How could a roc possibly learn to fly that quickly?
