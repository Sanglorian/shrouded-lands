Worshipped by the [Whispering Sisters](/wiki/whispering-sisters/). According to them, she was [Alberon](/wiki/alberon/)'s consort before he fell for - and married, and later divorced - the [Green Lady](/wiki/green-lady/). He spurned and entombed her.
