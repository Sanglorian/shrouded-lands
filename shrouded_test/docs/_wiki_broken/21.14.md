Birlwood Hold is a modest keep presided over by the [Knights of the Cudgel](/wiki/knights-of-the-cudgel/). Before the band of landless knights that would become the Cudgels seized this forest, it was home to the Birls, one of the [Witch Clans](/wiki/witch-clans/).
