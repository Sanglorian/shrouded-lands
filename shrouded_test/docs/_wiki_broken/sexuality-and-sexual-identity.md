# Sexuality
* Barrier Range boys sometimes travel to Simone's Aviary to see the bird that will predict their future wife, but often end up marrying one another ([Simone's Aviary](/wiki/the-city-itself/)).
* Those within Shuttered's Necromantic Office are often castrated.

# Sexual identity
* Gorean boys and girls have their coming of age rituals in the Glade of Womanhood ([07.18](/wiki/07-18/))
* In Gore, a woman may 'be a man whenever she needs'. [The Daughter of Ser Gareth Lemarr](/wiki/the-daughter-of-ser-gareth-lemarr/) cut her hair short in the Glade of Womanhood ([07.18](/wiki/07-18/)) to do just this
* Men use sex-change potions to meet with [Isane the Beauty](/wiki/isane-the-beauty/)
* Lady Anghart stole sex-change potions from Isane and intends to marry herself ([15.11](/wiki/15-11/))
* [Alberon](/wiki/alberon/) disguised himself as a woman to learn witchcraft off of the [Pacharia](/wiki/pacharia/) (see eleven recognised forms)
* Dwarves hide their gender and sex, to the extent that dwarves are identified as him-her.
* Orc women who want to take on male roles may shoulder the [Double Duty](/wiki/double-duty/).

# Sex
* The people of Northburn Holding believe that sex delays the coming of spring; to avoid having orcish babies foisted on them they may also resort to living only with members of the same sex ([22.03](/wiki/22-03/))
* [Gabber](/wiki/gabber/) is the language of travellers, circus folk and sexual 'deviants'

# Marriage
* A seawigged woman kills her husband by drowning on their marriage night.
* The village of Greyfaust has some peculiar marriage rituals ([08.05](/wiki/08-05/))
