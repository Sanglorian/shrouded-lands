A dwarven deity. The sister-brother of the [Drinker of Iron](/wiki/drinker-of-iron/). According to legend, when the Drinker of Iron cut off his-her head, the Speaker to Bronze's body sunk into the ground of Titan's Rest ([33.01](/wiki/33-01/)) and his-her head rolled to rest upon the mountain into which Titan's Skull is built ([33.00](/wiki/33-00/)).
