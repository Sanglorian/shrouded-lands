A **matriarch** is a female ruler of a faith, family or place. The [Matriarch](/wiki/matriarch/) is the head of the Church of Alberon. The Great Mother is the leader of the gnolls of the [Burning Lands](/wiki/burning-lands/). Previous Mothers were chosen by gnollish matriarchs. The [Nekh](/wiki/nekh/) are matriarchal. Marcila Lochgate is matriarch of the [Lochgates](/wiki/lochgates/), Maud Verlime is matriarch of the [Verlimes](/wiki/verlimes/), and the founding matriarch of Castle Karandur ([13.17](/wiki/13-17/)) was a bear.
