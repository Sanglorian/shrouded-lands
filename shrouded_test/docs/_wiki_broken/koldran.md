**Koldran the Mighty** was well-known in the north, particularly as the Holder of the Immovable Rod of Penultimate Thule (the North Pole) and as the father of many progeny (perhaps a hundred).

# Children
* Heleg Koldranson lounges around the Skibart holding ([20.03](/wiki/20-03/))

* Felix Koldranson, now known as [Flea](/wiki/flea/), leads a daggerfeet pack ([20.10](/wiki/20-10/))

* Grimkjel Koldranson leads a band of mercenary gnolls that the people of Northburn Holding ([22.03](/wiki/22-03/)) are considering hiring

* Tamilda Koldransdotter is [Azurnay](/wiki/azurnay/)'s best vermin wrangler
