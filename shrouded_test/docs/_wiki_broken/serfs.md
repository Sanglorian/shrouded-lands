The [Sanguine Lords](/wiki/sanguine-lords/) and the lords of Thring keep serfs. Other parts of the world may as well. The serfs of Tarengael pray to the [Green Lady](/wiki/green-lady/), but approach the [Sorcerer of Tarengael Town](/wiki/sorcerer-of-tarengael-town/) for darker jobs.
