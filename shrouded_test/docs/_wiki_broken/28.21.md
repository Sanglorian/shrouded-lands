[28.21](/wiki/28-21/)

Region: [Hinterlands of Blind Midshotgatepool](/wiki/hinterlands-of-blind-midshotgatepool/)

On dark nights the smells of delicious foods waft across this desolate stretch of beach. Those who approach can see a feast laid out on a wave-lapped table lit by bright candles, a blazing fire or even a chandelier. But approaching is not wise for those who do are liable to be consumed by a local mermaid named [Terise of Steadfast](/wiki/terise-of-steadfast/) ([20.13](/wiki/20-13/)).
