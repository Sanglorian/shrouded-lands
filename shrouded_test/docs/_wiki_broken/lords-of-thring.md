The **Lords of Thring** are the feudal lords of the [Duchy of Thring](/wiki/duchy-of-thring/). They answer ultimately to the [Duke of Thring](/wiki/duke-of-thring/). Each has their seat in a different castle, and typically their knights have different mounts (see also, mounts of Thring).
