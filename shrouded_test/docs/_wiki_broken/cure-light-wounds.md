A ***cure light wounds* potion** or **potion of *cure light wounds*** seems to be a standard magical measure. Sosarian beef jerky cured in giant bee honey has the effects of a *cure light wounds* potion ([50.14](/wiki/50-14/)). In Greymere Vale ([20.00](/wiki/20-00/)), a torrent of *cure light wounds* potion falls constantly from a tower.
