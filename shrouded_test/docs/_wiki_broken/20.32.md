The crabs that live exclusively in this black sinkhole several miles offshore from [Jahur](/wiki/jahur/) have [Abyss Diamonds](/wiki/abyss-diamonds/) on their backs.

Sadly, few who challenge the abyss return. Survivors tell of water pressure that crushes bones, and of horrific predators with blazing eyes, glowing tendrils and teeth like falchions. Kwalish met a water weird in the deeps, possibly here.
