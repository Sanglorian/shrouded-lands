The cataphracts are warriors of the [Golden Realm](/wiki/golden-realm/), crushed by the gnoll armies of the Great Mother and the orc armies of [Grumluda](/wiki/grumluda/) in the Battle of the Last River. They are described as "eagle-winged",  with the typical dark skin of southerners, brightly enameled steel and span-high hair.|thumb|400px|By Ptdtch, CC BY-SA.]]

[Elhanen](/wiki/elhanen/) the Silent has brought exiled cataphracts and blind lion priests to the [Shuttered City](/wiki/shuttered-city/).
