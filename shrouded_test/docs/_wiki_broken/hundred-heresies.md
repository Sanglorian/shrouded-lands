Dictated by the [Temple Indivisible](/wiki/temple-indivisible/) of the [Shuttered City](/wiki/shuttered-city/), which worships [Alberon](/wiki/alberon/)
# Attaching a fish or other sea-dwelling creature to the posterior by means of string or cord (see the [Keepers of the Sainted Foot](/wiki/keepers-of-the-sainted-foot/))
# Suggesting that [Shuttered's walls](/wiki/city-walls-(shuttered)/) are stable for any reason other than the fervent prayers of the priesthood
# Connections
* Along the [Street of Small Gods](/wiki/street-of-small-gods/), handmaidens and princes of Alberon offer 'services' that cleverly avoid violating the Heresies.
