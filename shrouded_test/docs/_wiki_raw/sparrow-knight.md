The '''Sparrow Knight''' is an unconventional [[knights of Thring|knight of Thring]]. He claims to know Sir [[Waine]]'s secrets and the source of that paladin's feather token. More traditional knights consider the Sparrow Knight's irreverent style an insult to Sir Waine, but the knight contends that Sir Waine was a radical in his day, creating the customs that are now considered timeless. 
==Connections==
*The knight once lost a Battle of the Hand ([[16.16]]) but survived by use of his [[feathers|feather token]]
*The knight has an explanation for the haunted statue ([[10.09]]) which involves beings of salt and chalk ([[09.09]]). He hopes telling that story will make [[Princess Elandra]] cry, earning him her hand in marriage.
*The knight fights in [[moon elephants|moon elephant]] leather
