* [[Quicklings]] dress in feathers.
* The [[Sorcerer of Tarengael Town]] specialises in featherstitch magic.
*The Sparrow Knight has a feather token which lets him float. He also claims that he knows the origin of [[Sir Waine and Tycell]]'s feather token, which also let them float.
*The traveller's journal (see [[Pirate Kings]]) begins with him clutching a white feather.
*The actress who was to play Ulena in [[The Song of Ban and Ulena]] awoke clutching a glowing feather.
*The people of Everdark ([[01.03]]) wear clothes woven from the long vanes of the feathers of great rocs. The clothes are waterproof, warm and comfortable.
*[[Geddar]] of the [[Goblin Markets]] offers all of her wealth to any who can bring her a tail feather of [[the mockingbird]].
*Lizardfolk trucked with beings of vine and feather.
*Dinosaur feathers are a [[ritual currency]].
*A pillow stuffed with [[cat feathers]] puts those who are muffled by it to sleep.
*[[Peacockatrices]]' feathers dazzle the mind of those who look at them.
*[[Firebirds]]' feathers give off a warm glow.
*[[Red-Feathers]] is a crowfolk outlaw.
*[[Asrigh]] forbids keeping feathered creatures in bondage.
*Doves on the Long Stairs ([[03.05]]) have their feathers died orange in honour of the Drinker of Iron.
*The ladies of [[Shuttered]] wear feathers.
*A feathered hairstyle among the dandies of [[Shuttered]] was inspired by [[Simone the Fowl]].
*[[Eliana]] inspired the residents of Shuttered to wear feathers, though few could match her firebird feather dresses, plucked so freshly that they still glowed.
*[[Painted elephants]] have brightly-coloured feathers and fur.
*The pointing bones used by the [[Howling College]] are wrapped in feathers.
