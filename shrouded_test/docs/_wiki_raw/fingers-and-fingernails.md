*The Market Pit is lit by candles made of human finger bones ([[28.04]])
*The mountain shepherds of Mt Scorshia eat human finger stew ([[02.04]])
*Lady Naideen is missing a finger ([[17.11]])
*The araneas of the Gloompatch flee men with a finger removed ([[23.08]])
*The Ferryman collects finger bone tolls ([[35.06]])
*[[Cloud halflings]] increasingly have six fingers or toes
*Master Var has made a ship in a bottle out of fingernails ([[43.03]])
*Ships from the Isle of Thalanyl are covered with human fingernails ([[Jahur]])
*[[Droesh]] keeps his lover's fingerbone around his neck.
*There was a [[War of the Six-Fingered Hand]]
*Those who wear the ring of the [[Honorable Society of Engineers]] without being a recognised member have their middle finger cut off.
