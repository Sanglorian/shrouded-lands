==Sexuality==
*Barrier Range boys sometimes travel to Simone's Aviary to see the bird that will predict their future wife, but often end up marrying one another ([[The City Itself#Simone's Aviary|Simone's Aviary]]).
*Those within Shuttered's Necromantic Office are often castrated.
==Sexual identity==
*Gorean boys and girls have their coming of age rituals in the Glade of Womanhood ([[07.18]])
*In Gore, a woman may 'be a man whenever she needs'. [[The Daughter of Ser Gareth Lemarr]] cut her hair short in the Glade of Womanhood ([[07.18]]) to do just this
*Men use sex-change potions to meet with [[Isane the Beauty]]
*[[Lady Anghart]] stole sex-change potions from Isane and intends to marry herself ([[15.11]])
*[[Alberon]] disguised himself as a woman to learn [[witches|witchcraft]] off of the [[Pacharia]] (see [[eleven recognised forms]])
*Dwarves hide their gender and sex, to the extent that dwarves are identified as him-her.
*Orc women who want to take on male roles may shoulder the [[Double Duty]].
==Sex==
*The people of Northburn Holding believe that sex delays the coming of spring; to avoid having orcish babies foisted on them they may also resort to living only with members of the same sex ([[22.03]])
*[[Gabber]] is the language of travellers, circus folk and sexual 'deviants'
==Marriage==
*A [[seawig]]ged woman kills her husband by drowning on their marriage night.
*The village of Greyfaust has some peculiar marriage rituals ([[08.05]])
