==Skeleton of a Great Bird, Sunken in Sand==
''Region:'' [[The Singing Wastes]]

Connects to: 06.03, 46.10 and 48.24
[[File:806.jp.jpg|thumb|400px|Artist's impression of a Ziz]]

Somewhere in the arid landscape of the [[Southern Desert]] there is to be found the skeleton of an enormous bird, with a wingspan of five hundred feet or more. In the shadow of its ribcage, small flowers grow, similar to those in the footprints of the [[Tarrasque]] but dark blue instead of yellow. Inside its skull, which is cracked as if from a mighty blow, there dwells a man, shaggy-bearded and ancient, bearing a talisman which displays the twin faces of [[King in Splendor|the Lion and the King]]. If questioned, he will claim to be the last keeper of the World's Edge Lighthouse ([[46.10]]).

All around the bird skeleton there are drawn images in the sand, the same two over and over. The first, which the hermit refers to as 'despair', is an image of a hammer; the latter, which he calls 'hope', is that of an egg.
