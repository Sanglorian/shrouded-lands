The '''Matriarch''' is the head of the Temple Indivisible and the bride of [[Alberon]]. 

==Details==
*The contract that allows the [[Temple of the Labyrinth]] to remain in the city was signed by the First Matriarch
*The personal chambers of the Matriarch are in the [[Steeple of Alberon]], below the [[Sealed Library]]. 
*The High Priestess of the Labyrinth is an Elector, making her the equal of the Matriarch or the head of any [[Great Family]]. 
*The Knights of the [[Canes Sanguis]] guard the Steeple of Alberon and the Matriarch. They answer only to their Grand Master, who is beholden to and appointed by the Matriarch and serves at her pleasure. 
*The Matriarch appears in [[With Morning Comes Mistfall]], where she says that it rains but once a year to remind all of the bounty of the freshwater [[Keening Sea]].

==Connections==
*The traditional duties of a wife include memorising the taboos set forth by the Matriarch and ensure that their households are in compliance with them. The larger a household a woman maintains the more difficult this becomes, which is perhaps part of the reason why the Doge has two wives. 
*The Matriarch banished the [[Devil's Bible]] to the Sealed Library. 
*When [[Simone the Fowl]] was accidentally elected doge, the College of Electors consulted with the Matriarch. 
*The [[Sealed Library]] is accessible only to the Matriarch and those she chooses.
