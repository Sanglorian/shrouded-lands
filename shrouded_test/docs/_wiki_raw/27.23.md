==Death's Lovelies==
[[File:2723.png|thumb|400px]]Hex 27.23

Connects to: 32.12.

This meagre camp around an ancient stone cairn is often uninhabited, for [[Death's Lovelies]] are a wandering bunch that travels all over the northwestern end of the Burning Lands, and up to Blind Midshotgatepool and the borders of Thring. When they are not around, the only things to be found here are a few red-lipped neophytes and piles of poisoned and cursed treasure.
