==Blessed are the Meek==
[[Alberon]], the story goes, desired the [[witches|witchcraft]] of the [[Pacharia]]. As a man, it was to him - as it is to [[Sexuality and sexual identity|all of his gender]] - denied. 

So Alberon passed out of the sight of men and returned as the Lady of Pain. He dressed as a sturdy fishwife and went unto the Pacharia to learn their ways.

Alberon was found out, and the Pacharia strung him up from a [[hawthorn]] plant. The [[Green Lady]] would or could not cut him down, but Alberon’s [[She Who Waits|first consort]] did - for a hideous price that has never been disclosed.

Still, Alberon has the womanly art of witchcraft, and he sometimes puts it to use, appearing as a beggar-woman and dispensing cures, love potions, talismans and hexes to those who show ‘her’ kindness. 

In fact, the itinerant is one of eleven recognised forms in which Alberon appears. The others are: the newly-wed, the squire, the [[goliards|goliard]], the handmaiden, the [[Doge]] (leading to some confusion, hijinx and paternity disputes), the [[elves|elf]]-catcher, the mother, the father, the widow and the Lord of Pain.

The similarity of these forms to the twelve gods of the [[Undying Cycle]] has not gone unnoticed, but the conclusions vary - is Alberon coopting the cycle, or paying homage to it, or somehow influenced by it outside of his control?
