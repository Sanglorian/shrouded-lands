The [[ice mummies]] of the Westmarches ([[03.02]]) are well-looted dead preserved by the cold. Some say they want revenge on grave looters. Their discarded skulls grin evilly, their skulls glow with strange runes on foggy nights and [[Algrael the Gelderer]] saw a lady in white only to end up in the embrace of an ice mummy. 
*[[Severard]] is a [[mellified mummies|mellified mummy]] waiting for the poison to be leached from his body. Meanwhile, only his brain is active.
