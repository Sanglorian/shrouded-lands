[[Wortimer]] of the [[Thieves guild (Blind Midshotgatepool)|Midshotgatepool thieves' guild]] wears a cloaker subdued by a clasp. If ambushed, Wortimer will remove the clasp and throw his cloak at an assailant. The cloaker will try to suffocate its victim.
