* *Alchemy** is a craft. While many [wizards](/wiki/wizards/) are **alchemists**, alchemy does not require spellcasting. 

## Practice
* The elementalists and alchemists of the [Idjits](/wiki/idjits/) can harness electricity to make hybrid creatures. They have weaponised [shock lizards](/wiki/shock-lizards/) and created [cloud mantas](/wiki/cloud-mantas/) capable of propelling ships.
* Alchemists discovered the main component of the [Scent Barrier](/wiki/scent-barrier/) in the caverns beneath Jahur. They create the pungent network of geysers that blasts a range of strong smells that makes those reliant on scent disoriented, then half-blind and insane.
* Quicksilver emulsifies certain magical potions, can cause unstable reactions, including twisting people to become [silverlings](/wiki/silverlings/). Some unscrupulous alchemists use silverling body parts in their formulae.
* Jahuri alchemists make blackpowder and fertiliser from the guano of sea birds.
* The [alka-lion](/wiki/alka-lion/) drips a powerful solvent sought out by alchemists ([14.27](/wiki/14-27/)).
* Master Var has developed a substance from ochre jelly that makes his skin mostly fireproof.
* Jiffon Soon created the [geas-eater](/wiki/geas-eater/) to remove from himself a powerful curse.
* An opportunistic alchemist captured a mating pair of [fire centipedes](/wiki/fire-centipedes/) ([17.30](/wiki/17-30/)).
* Orhan, in happier times, turned lead to gold. He also grew his own wives in vats and had a gnoll sing souls into them. While one of Orhan's wives had her body shattered in a recent attack, his apprentices have preserved the brain.
* *[The Lady](/wiki/the-lady/) grew a vat-creature, [Zor](/wiki/zor/).

## Alchemists
* [Master Var](/wiki/master-var/) is “something of” an alchemist.
* [Jiffon Soon](/wiki/jiffon-soon/), an exile of Naros, is a mad alchemist.
* The [Idjits](/wiki/idjits/)
* [Viceroy Orhan](/wiki/viceroy-orhan/) was chosen as treasurer because of his skill at alchemy.
* There is a gnome alchemist in [The Fearless](/wiki/the-fearless/).
* [Severard](/wiki/severard/) had an alchemy lab.
* Many alchemists now study alongside hexers and astrologers at the Imperial Academy of Thaumaturgy, which once taught wizards and sages.
