**Alchemy** is a craft. While many [[wizards]] are **alchemists**, alchemy does not require spellcasting. 

## Practice
*The elementalists and alchemists of the [[Idjits]] can harness electricity to make hybrid creatures. They have weaponised [[shock lizards]] and created [[cloud mantas]] capable of propelling ships. 
*Alchemists discovered the main component of the [[Scent Barrier]] in the caverns beneath Jahur. They create the pungent network of geysers that blasts a range of strong smells that makes those reliant on scent disoriented, then half-blind and insane.
*Quicksilver emulsifies certain magical potions, can cause unstable reactions, including twisting people to become [[silverlings]]. Some unscrupulous alchemists use silverling body parts in their formulae.  
*Jahuri alchemists make blackpowder and fertiliser from the guano of sea birds.
*The [[alka-lion]] drips a powerful solvent sought out by alchemists ([[14.27]]). 
*Master Var has developed a substance from ochre jelly that makes his skin mostly fireproof. 
*Jiffon Soon created the [[geas-eater]] to remove from himself a powerful curse.
*An opportunistic alchemist captured a mating pair of [[fire centipedes]] ([[17.30]]).
*Orhan, in happier times, turned lead to gold. He also grew his own wives in vats and had a gnoll sing souls into them. While one of Orhan's wives had her body shattered in a recent attack, his apprentices have preserved the brain. 
**[[The Lady]] grew a vat-creature, [[Zor]].

## Alchemists
*[[Master Var]] is “something of” an alchemist.
*[[Jiffon Soon]], an exile of Naros, is a mad alchemist.
*The [[Idjits]]
*[[Viceroy Orhan]] was chosen as treasurer because of his skill at alchemy. 
*There is a gnome alchemist in [[The Fearless]].
*[[Severard]] had an alchemy lab.
*Many alchemists now study alongside hexers and astrologers at the Imperial Academy of Thaumaturgy, which once taught wizards and sages.
