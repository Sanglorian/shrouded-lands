## The Missing Children of Millhaven
[[File:1714.png|thumb]]Connects to: 09.19, 11.01 and 14.02.

The quiet village of Millhaven would be unexceptional, but for the dozens of its children who have gone missing over the last few months. What the villagers do not know is that their children have fallen prey to several [[bugbears]], working for their dark master the Sack Man (09.19).
#### Hooks
*Have any brave **Duchy** knights investigated the disappearances? What happened to them?
