Connects to: [04.17](/wiki/04-17/), 5.20, [14.27.01](/wiki/14-27/) and [19.31.03](/wiki/19-31/).

This outdoor temple is ancient, and consists of two rings of standing stones that chart the movements of the sun, moon and stars. Some inner stones bear weathered reliefs of the androgynous deities known in Gore and [Jahur](/wiki/jahur/) as the Fools ([19.31](/wiki/19-31/).03). The site was sacred to the first settlers of Gore and [the High Kings of Gore](/wiki/the-high-kings-of-gore/) held it sacred too.

The High Kings of Gore were once crowned here. Today, local [centaurs](/wiki/centaurs/) ([04.17](/wiki/04-17/)) use the Fool's Court for their savage rites.
