Strange invisible assailants with form and substance.
* The Nothing ([04.31](/wiki/04-31/)) seems to have created invisible stalkers ([03.31](/wiki/03-31/)) in the Old Mill. The shamans say that they are souls regurgitated by the Nothing.
* *An intruder to the mill would see only six-toed footprints approaching through the powder before being slain.
* Attu of [Bones' Jangle](/wiki/bones'-jangle/) suspects that his cabaret is haunted, but rather than a restless spirit he has an invisible stalker controlled by [Imorcar](/wiki/imorcar/) and kept in a brass hookah. Imorcar releases it when he wants to eliminate a perceived threat.
