The [[Shuttered City]] embassies for three of these nations have been bricked up. One of those is Alacoda's; the other two have 'fallen to barbarism'. Naros' embassy is bricked up; it's unclear if it's counted among the two.
*[[Alacoda]], empire of the [[high elves]] and favoured of the [[Green Lady]]
*[[Golden Realm]] (pays tribute to the [[gnolls]] of the [[Burning Lands]])
*[[Grumluda]], domain of the Eternal-King-of-All-Orcs and importer of ferocious beasts
*[[Naros]] (fallen)
*[Minotaur nation] (conquered by orcs) ([[18.02]])
