## Overview
The land is barren here, in a narrow gap between the Keening Sea and the World's Edge. One of the few features of note is a small split in the dry earth, running northeast, into which has been inserted a huge wedge-shaped stone ten feet high. The point of the wedge is driven into the crack, while the flat top appears to have some sort of runes engraved upon it. If translated, the runes read:

: All shall be as it was in the beginning. Drowned [Andara](/wiki/andara/) again shall see the sun; black [Serpantia](/wiki/serpantia/), kingdom of worms, shall sleep beneath the waves.

: What [Melnir](/wiki/melnir/) ([51.12](/wiki/51-12/)) did shall be undone; what the Traitor Prince did shall be undone; what the Drowned await shall come to pass.

: Look to me when [the Hammer of Bronze](/wiki/the-hammer-of-bronze/) is forged anew. Look to me on the twelfth night when the sea boils. Look upon me, ye of Serpantia, and despair.
Sages say that the names Andara and Serpantia refer to ancient kingdoms, one of which was situated in the lands which are now [the Keening Sea](/wiki/the-keening-sea/), and the other in the lands beyond World's Edge. The meaning of the rest of the inscription is currently unknown.
