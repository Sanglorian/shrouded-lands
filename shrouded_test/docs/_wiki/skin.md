## Colour
*[Seline](/wiki/seline-and-eliana/) has very pale skin; [Eliana](/wiki/seline-and-eliana/) has golden skin despite her indoors lifestyle.

*The bartenders of [The Giant and the Gnome](/wiki/the-giant-and-the-gnome/) all have dark blue skin and claim to share a common ancestor.

*The [cataphracts](/wiki/cataphracts/) of [Elhanen](/wiki/elhanen/) (from the [Golden Realm](/wiki/golden-realm/)) have dark skin.

*[Silverlings](/wiki/silverlings/) can have their skin stained silver.
