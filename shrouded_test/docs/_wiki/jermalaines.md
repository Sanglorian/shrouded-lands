Jermalaines are finger-tall cave sprites who are almost as uncooperative and lazy as humans.

## Places
*Jermalaines inhabit Hearthwright Green ([19.01](/wiki/19-01/)), where they grow [mushrooms](/wiki/jermalaine-mushrooms/)
*[Albus Flidge](/wiki/albus-flidge/) enslaved some jermalaines from Hearthwright Green and uses them to create delicate clockwork ([29.14](/wiki/29-14/))
*One of Albus' jermalaines is whispering to the [Blind Doge](/wiki/blind-doge/)
