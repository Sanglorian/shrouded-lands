Connects to: [02.04](/wiki/02-04/), [29.07](/wiki/29-07/) and [36.04.02](/wiki/36-04/).

During misty nights a strange boat can be seen plying this stretch of the Witchwater ([29.07](/wiki/29-07/)): a cloaked figure poling a boat made of finger bones. The ferryman will take travelers across the river if they pay a finger and tell how they came by it. Freshly cut fingers and skeletal ones are both accepted, but those who lie or those who try to pay with a finger of a monkey (or other non-sentient species) will be cast into the water in the midstream.

Strangely enough, the ferryman has only ever been seen moving downriver.
### Connections:
* Some say that the Ferryman was once a receptacle for depressing elven memories, much like Amalar the Immaculate is today ([36.04.02](/wiki/36-04/)).
* Some mountain shepherds cook fingers in every pot of stew ([02.04](/wiki/02-04/)). What’s the deal with fingers?

### Hooks:
* Who is the ferryman? What does he (she? it?) want finger bones for?
* Does the ferryman carry anything in the boat except for travelers?
* How does the ferryman always move downstream but is always seen in this same stretch of the river?
