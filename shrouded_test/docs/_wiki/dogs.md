* The [Dogtur](/wiki/dogtur/) tame wild dogs ([04.08](/wiki/04-08/)). They are apparently born of the ur-dog, a hundred-teated bitch dog.
* The [Houndishmen](/wiki/houndishmen/) determine their ruler by fighting dogs.
* Children near [Ushcka](/wiki/ushcka/)'s lair ([21.15](/wiki/21-15/)) have been assigned dogs in the hopes that the animals will spot her trickery.
