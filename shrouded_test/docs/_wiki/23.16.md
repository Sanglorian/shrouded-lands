## Haunt of the Witch Queen
Hex 23.16

Connects to: *16.16*, *16.18,* 22.16, the Duchy of Thring, the August City of Blind Midshotgatepool and the City of Shuttered Windows.

[[The Witch Queen]] lives here, but whether she lives in towers of bone or a thatched house, or indeed what she looks like at all, is a matter of dispute. Her [[cauldronborn]] wander through the swamps with lanterns, leading lost souls to the Queen.
