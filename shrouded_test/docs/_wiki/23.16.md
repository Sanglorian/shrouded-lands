## Haunt of the Witch Queen
Hex [23.16](/wiki/23-16/)

Connects to: *[16.16](/wiki/16-16/)*, *[16.18](/wiki/16-18/),* [22.16](/wiki/22-16/), the Duchy of Thring, the August City of Blind Midshotgatepool and the City of Shuttered Windows.

[The Witch Queen](/wiki/the-witch-queen/) lives here, but whether she lives in towers of bone or a thatched house, or indeed what she looks like at all, is a matter of dispute. Her [cauldronborn](/wiki/cauldronborn/) wander through the swamps with lanterns, leading lost souls to the Queen.
