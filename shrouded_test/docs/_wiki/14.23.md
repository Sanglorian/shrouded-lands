Region: the Singing Wastes

## Overview
<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom:6.0pt;mso-para-margin-bottom:.5gd"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:9.0pt;font-family:"CenturyGothic","sans-serif"; color:black;mso-themecolor:text1">Among the lone and level sands of this stretch of the Singing Wastes lie the ruins of a great hot air balloon. It set out from Shuttered some time ago carrying three noble exiles and their flesh golem minders from the City. </span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom:6.0pt;mso-para-margin-bottom:.5gd"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:9.0pt;font-family:"CenturyGothic","sans-serif"; color:black;mso-themecolor:text1">The three flesh golems (29.14.35) patrol the downed craft even now, carrying great mechanical crossbows that they shoot at any intruder (as some hungry local ghouls have learned to their cost). Two of the prisoners are still securely bound but the third was fed to the other two after food supplies ran out. </span></p>
If approached the two prisoners will offer anything they can think of in return for rescue.
