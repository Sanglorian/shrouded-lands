## Seven Wonders of the World
The scholar and rakish traveller [[Taran Prenderghast]] has seen recent success with a book proposing Seven Wonders of the Modern World. 

Published in the [[Shuttered City]] under the patronage of [[Blind Doge|the Doge]], the "world" seems limited to the Shrouded Lands and the [[Twelve Nations]], and particularly those parts that Taran happens to have visited.

His list is:
# The [[White Road]], and the great bindings of spirits that it represents.
# [[The Giant and the Gnome]]. The draft list gave the Great Temple of Alberon instead, but rumour has it that Taran was offered free drinks till his dying day if he changed the entry.
# The [[Waterworks (Shuttered)|Waterworks of Shuttered]], which drowned the City's enemies. 
# The gates of [[Hoth Achaar]], whcih shift as the fortress' population does.
# The stump of [[Ninbolm]].
# [[51.12|Melnir's Mount]].
# [[50.30|Teodo]], originally the only wonder outside of the Shrouded Lands, although thanks to the Great Mother, this has changed since publication.

Taran's list is a revision of a list compiled over a thousand years ago by the fabled philosopher-knight [[Caprondes]]. Caprondes' list consisted of:
# Melnir's Mount, the only ancient wonder that remains to this day. Taran considered dropping it from this list, but concedes that it remains evidence of an impressive feat.
# The [[Tarrasque]] of [[Bergolast]]. The wonder survives but the city that it supported does not. 
# The [[Great Oak of Goblinkind]]. In a manner of speaking, this wonder also made it onto Taran's list.
# The Spear-in-the-Stone of [[Adherion]].
# The Heliotrope of the [[Well-Lit Arch]]. The Well-Lit Arch was a city in the [[Golden Realm]].
# The [[Temple of Elemental Good]] in [[Othonoi]] (this may be what is now referred to in hushed voices as the "[[49.11|Terminus Temple]]"). 
# The [[Mausoleum of Khaldi]]. Khaldi existed in both [[Zamorqy]] and [[Zhuriman]].
