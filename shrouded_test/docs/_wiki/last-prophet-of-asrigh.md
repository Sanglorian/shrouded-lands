<span style="text-align:left;">A revival of the worship of </span>[[Asrigh]]<span style="text-align:left;"> has begun among the farmers of </span>[[27.18]]<span style="text-align:left;">, though they call him Saray. Giaccamo, a disgruntled bishop of Alberon, read a forbidden text of Asrigh while in the Sealed Library (</span>[[29.14]]<span style="text-align:left;">.50). Shortly after, he received a vision in which Asrigh rose from his prison and brought rain back to the Shrouded Lands (</span>[[29.14]]<span style="text-align:left;">.36). He denounced Alberon, faked his death and changed his face. The ex-bishop now calls himself </span>[[Thoth]]<span style="text-align:left;">, and he evangelizes to farmers around the </span>[[Keening Sea]]<span style="text-align:left;">, never staying in one place for more than a night. He works in hope that Asrigh will one day rise and make good on his vision. </span>

## Connection
*[[Wortimer]] ([26.20.01](/wiki/26-20/)) supports Thoth, hoping he can undermine the Temple Indivisible's influence in the August City.
