According to legend, when the Queen Sinister left the [Bloodied King](/wiki/bloodied-king/) and his court she cursed the Shrouded Lands so that it now only rains once a year (described in [With Morning Comes Mistfall](/wiki/with-morning-comes-mistfall/)).

# Connections
* A tempest, a powerful elemental, was bound to a stone spire in the [Singing Wastes](/wiki/singing-wastes/) in the hopes that it would provide rain, but it is under the thumb of the Queen Sinister.

* Those seelie elves who joined them quarrelled with the Queen, were abandoned in the [Singing Wastes](/wiki/singing-wastes/), and went on to found [Zaal](/wiki/zaal/), the Sleeping City (they are now known as the white elves). 

* When [Grandfather Cactus](/wiki/grandfather-cactus/) joined others petitioning the Bloodied King for the return of the rains, he was granted the companionship of a nymph as a boon - though it is said that it was the Queen, and not the King, who banished the rains.
* The [Bastard Prince](/wiki/bastard-prince/) is apparently the son of the Queen's favourite daughter and the [Bloodied King](/wiki/bloodied-king/)'s eldest son.
