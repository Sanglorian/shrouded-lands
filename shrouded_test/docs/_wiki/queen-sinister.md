[[File:Salomé-_a_tragedy_in_one_act_pg_29.jpg|thumb|400px]]When the elven court in the [[Kingswood]] split, the exiled renegades (most of the [[unseelie elves]] and some [[seelie elves]]) were led away by the Queen Sinister.

According to legend, when the Queen Sinister left the [[Bloodied King]] and his court she cursed the Shrouded Lands so that it now only [[rain]]s once a year (described in [[With Morning Comes Mistfall]]).

## Connections
*A [[tempests|tempest]], a powerful elemental, was bound to a stone spire in the [[Singing Wastes]] in the hopes that it would provide rain, but it is under the thumb of the Queen Sinister.

*Those seelie elves who joined them quarrelled with the Queen, were abandoned in the [[Singing Wastes]], and went on to found [[Zaal]], the Sleeping City (they are now known as the [[white elves]]). 

*When [[Grandfather Cactus]] joined others petitioning the Bloodied King for the return of the rains, he was granted the companionship of a [[nymphs|nymph]] as a boon - though it is said that it was the Queen, and not the King, who banished the rains.
*The [[Bastard Prince]] is apparently the son of the Queen's favourite daughter and the [[Bloodied King]]'s eldest son.
