==Birlwood Hold==
[[File:2114.png|thumb|400px]]Connects to: 19.15, 23.16, 29.14.35, 29.14.40 and the Barrier Range.

Birlwood Hold is a modest keep presided over by the [[Knights of the Cudgel]]. Before the band of landless knights that would become the Cudgels seized this forest, it was home to the Birls, one of the [[Witch Clans]].
