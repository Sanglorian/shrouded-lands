All tales agree that the [[Witch Queen]] has a cauldron of black iron, large enough for three to bathe within. Bodies placed into the cauldron reanimate, although whether they are quite the same as they were before is unclear. Many of the cauldronborn, their skin pale and waxy, wander through the swamps with lanterns, leading lost souls to the Queen.
