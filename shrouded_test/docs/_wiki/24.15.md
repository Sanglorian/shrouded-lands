[24.15](/wiki/24-15/)

* *Region: **[The Barrier Range](/wiki/the-barrier-range/)

The domain of Clan Yarbeen is so obscure that many reject it as nothing but a cartographer's error, but a few people who have managed to climb up the peaks of this stretch of the Barrier Range report seeing a secluded mountain valley with sturdy goats herded by children and well-built cabins wreathed in wood smoke. However, those who tried to approach the valley found themselves transported immediately on the far side of the valley, sometimes on the top of a cliff, sometimes half of the way down one. Eventually they grew too frustrated or too dead to try to gain entrance into the valley.
