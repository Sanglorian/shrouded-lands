*Most of the [aarakocra](/wiki/aarakocra/) clan the [Kagu](/wiki/kagu/) speak only their own language.
*There is someone singing in [Demonic](/wiki/demonic/) ([05.05](/wiki/05-05/))
*The [Ghoul in the Gilded Cage](/wiki/ghoul-in-the-gilded-cage/) speaks in [Gnollish](/wiki/gnollish/).
*The [Mud Tongue and High Tongue](/wiki/mud-tongue-and-high-tongue/) are spoken in Shuttered. 
**The Mud Tongue is known outside of Shuttered as the Common Tongue. The Mud Tongue is the language of the common people of Shuttered. The very richest and youngest of the Shuttered nobility often only speak the High Tongue. A degenerate dialect of the Mud Tongue is spoken by the [wild men](/wiki/wild-men/). 
**All legal instruction in sorcery in the Shuttered City is carried out in the High Tongue and while most every noble is capable of producing at least a few [cantrips](/wiki/cantrips/), the common people cannot even understand the names of the [Thousand Spells and One](/wiki/thousand-spells-and-one/).
*[Gabber](/wiki/gabber/) spoken by circus folk, travellers, sexual "deviants" and [Scarecrows](/wiki/scarecrows/).
