## The Ruins of Maddlow Castle
Region: [The Barrier Range](/wiki/the-barrier-range/)

<p class="western" lang="en-US" style="margin-bottom:0.14in">Connects to [24.13](/wiki/24-13/), [25.17](/wiki/25-17/), *[26.20](/wiki/26-20/), [29.14.17](/wiki/29-14/)* and the **Duchy of Thring**.</p>

<p class="western" lang="en-US" style="margin-bottom:0.14in">In a high valley at the center of the Witch Clans' domains, there is a place that they call a ruin but is more accurately the opposite of a ruin - a castle that was only ever half built and then abandoned. Long ago, this place was the seat of power for [The Maddlows](/wiki/the-maddlows/).</p>

<p class="western" lang="en-US" style="margin-bottom:0.14in">The reason that the castle was never fully built is that each Maddlow suspected the others of inserting secret doors and traps into the design.</p>
#### Connection
*<p lang="en-US" style="margin-bottom:0.14in">[Gischard Penderghast](/wiki/gischard-penderghast/) ([24.13](/wiki/24-13/)) looted Maddlow Castle some time ago and left its walls perilously full of holes.</p>
