The [River of Crystal Waters](/wiki/river-of-crystal-waters/) passes through this hex. [Castle Tarengael](/wiki/castle-tarengael/) is built on a rocky island in the middle of the river. Bridges connect the castle to the settlements of Eastbridge and Westbridge, which are together referred to as [Tarengael Town](/wiki/tarengael-town/). Around are fields for crops, and behind a ridge is the [Swamp of Champions](/wiki/swamp-of-champions/), a key part of trial by combat in this area.

* The Swamp of Champions: ritual dueling is better with trebuchets. This is the final destination of the loser of a [Battle of the Hand](/wiki/battle-of-the-hand/)

### Connections:
* An old watchtower was once built to protect the borders of **Thring** ([20.18](/wiki/20-18/)) but it is now inhabited by the Foolish Sages who were exiled by the Duke.
