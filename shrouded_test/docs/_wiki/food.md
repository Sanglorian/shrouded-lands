*The orc army marches on its [[potatoes and pies]]
*Two [[elemental beings]] are associated with food:
**Chelind's buns ([[16.16]]) seem to be enchanted by a being of dew and vapour
**A being of pollen and milk is enslaved to create food by the proprietor of the finest restaurant in [[the Cross]]
*[[Terise of Steadfast]] is a mermaid who can create fine food, but only eat human flesh.
*The [[swine-harpies]] devour or defecate on any food brought into Swine Ravine ([[12.22]])
*[[Severard]] had a spell, *Severard's piquant premonition*, which let a chef learn the reaction to his or her meals
*Abdul Zassiz' meals provide flavour and nutrition - the flavour comes from illusions, the nutrition from food that most people would prefer not to eat ([[05.04]].
*Keith's southern Fried Entrails (KFC) serves tripe cooked on magical stones using the Jahuri style - made of the entrails of those trapped by a giant spider ([[15.07]])
*The [[King in Splendour]] is tempted by - among other things - food and drink as he passes through the underworld each night, according to the [[Testament of Wenneslas Stannev]]
*Someone who kills a white tiger of the Imperium (see [[tigers]]) has all food he or she eats develop the taste and consistency of ash.
*Citizens of Shuttered eat ostrich and cattle meat, but mostly crops grown in the Gardens of the Sea ([[29.15]])
*Food cooked in the cauldron of Galovain is tripled in mass, one of several effects of the magic item (see [[Golden Light]])
*Deep dwarves were feeding their goblin slaves trollflesh from a still-living troll before [[Master Var]] rescued it.
