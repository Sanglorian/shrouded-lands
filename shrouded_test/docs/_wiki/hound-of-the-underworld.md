According to the [[Chant of Morning]]:

:The [[Lion in Splendor]] went down through the gates of darkness and the arteries of the earth. He saw the black water of the [[Sunless Sea]], rise up, bubbling and boiling. But he commanded the woman’s hound to lap at the water and the Lion walked on with dry feet and the hound laps still, his [[ur-dog|bitch]] ([[04.08]]) crying in darkness for its mate. But as the Lion passed the hound’s teeth tore away his mane.
