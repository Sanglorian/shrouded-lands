According to the [Chant of Morning](/wiki/chant-of-morning/):

:The [Lion in Splendor](/wiki/lion-in-splendor/) went down through the gates of darkness and the arteries of the earth. He saw the black water of the [Sunless Sea](/wiki/sunless-sea/), rise up, bubbling and boiling. But he commanded the woman’s hound to lap at the water and the Lion walked on with dry feet and the hound laps still, his [bitch](/wiki/ur-dog/) ([04.08](/wiki/04-08/)) crying in darkness for its mate. But as the Lion passed the hound’s teeth tore away his mane.
