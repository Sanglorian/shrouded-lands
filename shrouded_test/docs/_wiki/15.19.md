Here lie the simple tombstones of [Sir Waine and his squire Tycel](/wiki/sir-waine/), two of the bravest paladins ever to raise sword in the service of Duke and Lady. The graves are heavily marked by hammer and chisel for the knights of **Thring** hold it to be lucky to place a fragment of the two stones on either side of their sword pommels, but they still stand together as the two did in life.
