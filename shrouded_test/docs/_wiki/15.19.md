## The Graves of Heroes
[[File:1519.png|thumb]]Connects to: 15.15 and 32.22.

Here lie the simple tombstones of [[Sir Waine|Sir Waine and his squire Tycel]], two of the bravest paladins ever to raise sword in the service of Duke and Lady. The graves are heavily marked by hammer and chisel for the knights of **Thring** hold it to be lucky to place a fragment of the two stones on either side of their sword pommels, but they still stand together as the two did in life.
