![](/media/Odilon_Redon_-_The_Monster.jpg)Traces of the vampire hordes that once swept across the Shrouded Lands still remain in the Vampiric Stratum ([[02.02]]).

The [[Lords Sanguine]] have some vampiric traits, and they do drink [[Tarrasque]] blood. [[Stirges]] are also bloodsuckers.

The [[Bergolasti vampire frogs|Bergolasti vampire frog]] ([[50.26]]) is so-named because it is parasitic; it does not appear to be a true vampire.

[[Bloodwretches]] have the vampire's traditional weaknesses, but not its strengths. They subsist on 'rosewater', ersatz blood.

A vampire hangs in [[Jahur]], the subject of a failed execution.
## Vampires
