![](/media/Odilon_Redon_-_The_Monster.jpg)Traces of the vampire hordes that once swept across the Shrouded Lands still remain in the Vampiric Stratum ([02.02](/wiki/02-02/)).

The [Lords Sanguine](/wiki/lords-sanguine/) have some vampiric traits, and they do drink [Tarrasque](/wiki/tarrasque/) blood. [Stirges](/wiki/stirges/) are also bloodsuckers.

The [Bergolasti vampire frog](/wiki/bergolasti-vampire-frogs/) ([50.26](/wiki/50-26/)) is so-named because it is parasitic; it does not appear to be a true vampire.

[Bloodwretches](/wiki/bloodwretches/) have the vampire's traditional weaknesses, but not its strengths. They subsist on 'rosewater', ersatz blood.

A vampire hangs in [Jahur](/wiki/jahur/), the subject of a failed execution.
## Vampires
