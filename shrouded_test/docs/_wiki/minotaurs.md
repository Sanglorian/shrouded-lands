Minotaurs (the name is irregularly capitalised) are [chimerics](/wiki/chimerics/), creations of [Chimalia](/wiki/chimalia/). The minotaur nation was formerly one of the [Twelve Nations](/wiki/twelve-nations/), but a couple of generations ago [Grumluda](/wiki/grumluda/), [Naros](/wiki/naros/) and the [merfolk](/wiki/merfolk/) of the [Southside Ocean](/wiki/southside-ocean/) conspired to destroy it. Minotaurs were decreed to be as they were when the world was young - slaves to the orcish Eternal-King in life and fodder to be reanimated in death. What were once minotaur lands were absorbed by the conquerors.

A grouping of minotaurs in a steading is a herd, and the minotaurs of Lastmaze are led by a bull.

Shuttered has signed the [Horn Treaty](/wiki/horn-treaty/), decreeing all minotaurs to be property of the orcish nation. The bull-like minotaurs were always considered semi-heretical by the church of [Alberon](/wiki/alberon/), which sacrifices white cattle.
