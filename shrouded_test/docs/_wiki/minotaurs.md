Minotaurs (the name is irregularly capitalised) are [[chimerics]], creations of [[Chimalia]]. The minotaur nation was formerly one of the [[Twelve Nations]], but a couple of generations ago [[Grumluda]], [[Naros]] and the [[merfolk]] of the [[Southside Ocean]] conspired to destroy it. Minotaurs were decreed to be as they were when the world was young - slaves to the orcish Eternal-King in life and fodder to be reanimated in death. What were once minotaur lands were absorbed by the conquerors.

A grouping of minotaurs in a steading is a herd, and the minotaurs of Lastmaze are led by a bull.

Shuttered has signed the [[Horn Treaty]], decreeing all minotaurs to be property of the orcish nation. The bull-like minotaurs were always considered semi-heretical by the church of [[Alberon]], which sacrifices white cattle.
