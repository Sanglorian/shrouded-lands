The Browncloaks include at least [Elwen](/wiki/elwen/) the Snickersnee and her companions, a one-legged gnomish illusionist, a crowfolk ratter and a masked deep dwarven machinist. They are drinking in Farnsfall ([11.03](/wiki/11-03/)) and claim to have looted a hidden dragon cave - leaving behind mountains of copper pennies ([08.01](/wiki/08-01/)).
