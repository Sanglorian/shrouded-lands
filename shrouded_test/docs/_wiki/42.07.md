## The West Reach

## Overview
This marks the western boundary of the World’s Edge and from here it sweeps away to the north and southeast. It is also here that the limestone cliff of the edge of the world contains more cracks than elsewhere, making attempting to climb down it less suicidal here than elsewhere.
