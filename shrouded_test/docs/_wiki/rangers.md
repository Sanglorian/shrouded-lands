The Marsh Rangers were an organisation that patrolled Yrump Marsh ([[26.19]]) until [[Blind Midshotgatepool]] succumbed to the [[Shuttered City]] and they were disbanded. The marsh was claimed by bullywugs that had been living in the August City's slums. 

[[Grondelgar Lochgate]] is captain of rangers in [[Winds]].
