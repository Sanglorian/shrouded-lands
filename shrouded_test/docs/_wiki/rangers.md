The Marsh Rangers were an organisation that patrolled Yrump Marsh ([26.19](/wiki/26-19/)) until [Blind Midshotgatepool](/wiki/blind-midshotgatepool/) succumbed to the [Shuttered City](/wiki/shuttered-city/) and they were disbanded. The marsh was claimed by bullywugs that had been living in the August City's slums.

Grondelgar Lochgate is captain of rangers in [Winds](/wiki/winds/).
