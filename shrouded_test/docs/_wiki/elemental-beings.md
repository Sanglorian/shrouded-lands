There are a number of beings of X and Y.

*The being of fire and smoke ([[03.04]], [[04.06]]). Currently appears to be suppressed, but [[Father Dorek]] thought that the being was an avatar of the [[Drinker of Iron]].
*Spirits of smoke and flame animated the Fist of Madai ([[34.28]])
*The being of dew and vapour ([[16.16]]). Associated with food.

*The being of pollen and milk ([[02.11]]). Associated with food.
*The being of soot and ash ([[04.02]], [[09.09]]).
*The being of rime and hoarfrost ([[39.30]]). Longs to be melted.
*A creature of flame and shadow, summoned by the [[Bloodied King]] to destroy the [[dragonblooded]] trees of the Stolen Hills ([[24.11]])
*Beings of salt and chalk lived in the Forgotten City-State and one now dwells in the Freeholds ([[09.09]])
*[[Lizardfolk]] shamans begun the art of [[pactmaking]] by trucking with beings of dust and wind, of vine and feather and of chalk and salt ([[33.04]])
