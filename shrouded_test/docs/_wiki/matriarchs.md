A **matriarch** is a female ruler of a faith, family or place. The [Matriarch](/wiki/matriarch/) is the head of the [Church of Alberon](/wiki/church-of-alberon/). The [Great Mother](/wiki/great-mother/) is the leader of the [gnolls](/wiki/gnolls/) of the [Burning Lands](/wiki/burning-lands/). Previous [Mothers](/wiki/mothers/) were chosen by gnollish matriarchs. The [Nekh](/wiki/nekh/) are matriarchal. [Marcila Lochgate](/wiki/marcila-lochgate/) is matriarch of the [Lochgates](/wiki/lochgates/), [Maud Verlime](/wiki/maud-verlime/) is matriarch of the [Verlimes](/wiki/verlimes/), and the founding matriarch of Castle Karandur ([13.17](/wiki/13-17/)) was a bear.
