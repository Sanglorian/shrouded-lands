A **matriarch** is a female ruler of a faith, family or place. The [[Matriarch]] is the head of the [[Church of Alberon]]. The [[Great Mother]] is the leader of the [[gnolls]] of the [[Burning Lands]]. Previous [[Mothers]] were chosen by gnollish matriarchs. The [[Nekh]] are matriarchal. [[Marcila Lochgate]] is matriarch of the [[Lochgates]], [[Maud Verlime]] is matriarch of the [[Verlimes]], and the founding matriarch of Castle Karandur ([[13.17]]) was a bear.
