==The Boneyard==
[[File:3827.png|thumb|358px]]Hex 38.27

Connects to: 38.26, 38.28 and 40.20.

North of the ruins of the city of Bergolast (38.28) lies the Boneyard in which thousands of shards of Tarrasque bone have been driven into the ground, some small others several spans long. The pieces of bone still shine white, whiter than ever in fact due to the droppings of the flocks of [[vultures]] that nest here. The vultures are well-fed thanks to the presence of the nearby Gibbering Hills ([[38.26]]).
===Connection:===
*The people of Hyfalls ([[40.20]]) have mounted shards of Tarrasque bone on their walls to ward off gnolls.
===Hook:===
*Why did the people of Bergolast stick all of those pieces of Tarrasque bone into the ground?
