[43.08](/wiki/43-08/)

[The Witchwater](/wiki/the-witchwater/), which begins with a geyser that bursts from the earth before the gates of the Holt of the Bloodied King ([29.07](/wiki/29-07/)) flows through the eastern parts of the Kingswood before finally falling off of the World’s Edge in a great waterfall of breathtaking beauty.

Next to the great waterfall rises the tall ivory tower of the elven wizard named Palendar who is now known as [The Weeper](/wiki/the-weeper/). Since the disappearance of his daughter he has not set foot outside his tower and his sobs echo up and down the waterfall, their sound strangely amplified and intermingled with that of the fall.

These days few are allowed within his tower to speak with him and the overly-persistent are driven off lamenting.
