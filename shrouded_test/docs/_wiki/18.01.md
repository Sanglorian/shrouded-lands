## The Lair of the Ostrlich
<p style="text-align:left;">The site of the Last Skirmish ([[17.03]]) is known by all to be accursed and haunted by the ghosts of men, [[halflings]] and [[ostriches]]. Yet fewer men are aware of the most terrible undead ostrich of all - the immortal [[ostrlich]] who dwells here in an empty barrow, to the north of the battlefield.</p>
