* *Region:** [The Grey Mountains](/wiki/the-grey-mountains/)

Connects to: [17.03](/wiki/17-03/) and [17.05](/wiki/17-05/).

The site of the Last Skirmish ([17.03](/wiki/17-03/)) is known by all to be accursed and haunted by the ghosts of men, [halflings](/wiki/halflings/) and [ostriches](/wiki/ostriches/). Yet fewer men are aware of the most terrible undead ostrich of all - the immortal [ostrlich](/wiki/ostrlich/) who dwells here in an empty barrow, to the north of the battlefield.

See [ostrlich](/wiki/ostrlich/) for more, including details of the barrow.
