[18.01](/wiki/18-01/)

**Region:** [The Grey Mountains](/wiki/the-grey-mountains/)

The site of the Last Skirmish ([17.03](/wiki/17-03/)) is known by all to be accursed and haunted by the ghosts of men, halflings and ostriches. Yet fewer men are aware of the most terrible undead ostrich of all - the immortal ostrlich who dwells here in an empty barrow, to the north of the battlefield.

See ostrlich for more, including details of the barrow.
