![](/media/Edward_Burne-Jones_-_Perseus.jpeg)A grey worm is found in its lair ([[46.06]]). They are known for their rapacious greed and animal cunning, and can swallow a [[su-giraffes|su-giraffe]] whole - which satiates it for a fortnight.

A fossiled grey worm is caught in battle with a [[burning eagles|burning eagle]] in the Fernsbank Fossil ([[02.10]]). The Stern Way associates the worm with water. The worm has a larger cousin, which is [[purple worms|purple]]
