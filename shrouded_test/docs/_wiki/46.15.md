## Overview
The nameless crayfish god ([48.13](/wiki/48-13/)) is not the only danger of the chill grey waters of the eastern end of the Keening Sea. Far from established trade routes is a hilly isle that the local fishermen are careful to avoid where [The Heget](/wiki/the-heget/) and her sons live.
