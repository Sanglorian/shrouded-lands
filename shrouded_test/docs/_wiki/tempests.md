A powerful elemental that is essentially a living storm.

## Tempests
*A tempest was bound to the Spire of Idjit ([19.31](/wiki/19-31/)) in the hopes that it would provide rain, but it is under the thumb of the [Queen Sinister](/wiki/queen-sinister/) and does not do so. It is used to execute prisoners and the [Idjits](/wiki/idjits/) harness its lightning for various alchemical and elementalist purposes. The tempest produces tempest spawn, but the Idjits believe that this is a result of tampering with the tempest, not a natural process.
