## The Haunt of the Mud Dragons
**Region:** [[the Devil's Fingers]].

Connects to: 16.20, 17.18, 20.18 and 41.24.

## Overview
[[Mud dragons]] are native to lands far to the south of the Shrouded Lands. But centuries ago, a small [[lizardfolk|lizardman]] ([[41.24) caravan carrying a breeding pair of these foul lizards was destroyed in a battle with the halflings of Jar Town ([[16.20]]). The beasts escaped the melee early and returned in the aftermath to feast on their fallen masters. Generations of their inbred offspring have flourished in this valley ([[16.23]]).
