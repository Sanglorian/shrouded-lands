## Overview
[Mud dragons](/wiki/mud-dragons/) are native to lands far to the south of the Shrouded Lands. But centuries ago, a small [lizardman](/wiki/lizardfolk/) ([41.24) caravan carrying a breeding pair of these foul lizards was destroyed in a battle with the halflings of Jar Town ([[16.20](/wiki/4124-caravan-carrying-a-breeding-pair-of-these-foul-lizards-was-destroyed-in-a-battle-with-the-halflings-of-jar-town-1620/)). The beasts escaped the melee early and returned in the aftermath to feast on their fallen masters. Generations of their inbred offspring have flourished in this valley ([16.23](/wiki/16-23/)).
