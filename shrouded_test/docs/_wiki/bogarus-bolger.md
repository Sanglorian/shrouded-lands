A rising member of the bosses that run the Wedding Band ([[30.15]]), **Bogarus Bolger** is a [[halflings|halfling]]. Once famous for capturing and supplying a number of strange beasts for the games, he has now settled down to run his [[blink dogs|blink dog]] kennel and other business interests.

Bolger's manner can be disarming, for he acts much like a gentleman halfling farmer from the Freeholds and is always unfailingly polite. He often apologizes to his victims before feeding them to his henchman, an uncommonly cunning [[trolls|troll]] of great and varied hungers (Bolger generally pays him in the bodies of exotic creatures and gladiators killed in the games and he is steadfastly loyal to his employer and always hungry). Currently Bolger is in the market for captured [[leucrotta]] ([[04.07]]) and is willing to pay handsomely for them.

### Connections
*One of the victims of Bogarus Bolger ([[Armand of the Axe]]) currently lives in one of the tombs along the White Road ([[29.13]].01).
*Bogarus originally hails from the Bolger Freehold ([[11.08]]).
*Bogarus may have gotten his blink dogs from the Haunted Isle ([[16.15]]).
*Bogarus is not pleased with the vampiric activites of his cousin, but he has not yet moved against her ([[29.14]].44).

## Bogarus' Safe Escape
[[File:301502.png|thumb|300px]]Hex 30.15.02

This spell allows the caster to escape otherwise fatal circumstances. It teleports the caster 1d100 x 10 feet in a random direction. If this results in the caster being placed within a solid object then they instead appear in the nearest open space. However, if the caster appears above ground level then they will fall as normal.

Bogarus Bolger has had his brooch enchanted so that when its pin claims a drop of his blood this spell is triggered. Despite this spell’s massive unpredictability, it has allowed Bogarus to escape for several sticky situations.
