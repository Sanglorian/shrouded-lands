# Aarakocra
* The [Kagu](/wiki/kagu/) are a major, perhaps the only, aarakocra clan.

# Connections
* Related to crowfolk, with whom they share a love of shiny things.
* The [Doge](/wiki/doge/) himself owns a number of aarakocra slaves that serve as bodyguards, servants and dancers. They can often be seen wheeling above the city‘s spires (or around the balloons).
