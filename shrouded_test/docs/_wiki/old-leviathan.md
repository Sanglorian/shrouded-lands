|thumb|400px|Gustave Doré]]According to the lion priests, at the end of days the Lion in Splendour will come before them, dragging the body of Old Leviathan behind Him. By the time He has brought it before them, it will have cooked in the radiant heat that marks the end of days. His claws will slice through Old Leviathan’s thick skin and out will pour steaming blubber and tender meat. The faithful will eat well. (See [06.03](/wiki/06-03/) for more)
