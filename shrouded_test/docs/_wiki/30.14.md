Hex [30.14](/wiki/30-14/)


Here, not far from the tombs that escort the [White Road](/wiki/white-road/) out of the [City of Shuttered Windows](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/) is a small statue of the demigod of disease, mutilation and torment known as [the Azru](/wiki/the-azru/). 

As far as this particular statue, it is under constant guard by order of the Doge himself in order to prevent local beggars from drinking its tears and thereby contracting a remarkably hideous (but not particularly painful) skin ailment.
### Hooks:
* Which beggars are trying particularly hard to drink the tears of the statue of the Azru? Do they have enough money to pay adventurers to gather some disease-causing tears?
* Does anyone else want those tears?
