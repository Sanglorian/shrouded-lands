*[Wax golems](/wiki/wax-golems/) take a number of forms, with different powers.
*[Clay golems](/wiki/clay-golems/)
*[Bone golems](/wiki/bone-golems/)
**[Gatherer-of-Bones](/wiki/gatherer-of-bones/) is one consciousness that inhabits four identical bone golems.
**A collective of bone golems sifts through the Bone Heap ([50.03](/wiki/50-03/))
*[Flesh golems](/wiki/flesh-golems/)
**Shuttered noble exiles are accompanied  by flesh golems created by the [Necromantic Office](/wiki/necromantic-office/).
**It's not clear if flesh golems are a type of [undead](/wiki/undead/).
**[Sorlak the Gelderer](/wiki/sorlak-the-gelderer/)'s essay on the races of the Shrouded Lands is usually translated as “The Flesh Golems that Live as Easily as They Die.” It describes how men, dwarves and all the rest are fleshy automatons driven by their nature and the impulses of their environment and how lizardmen can take advantage of this.
*The [Fist of Madai](/wiki/fist-of-madai/) is a colossal humanoid figure shaped by [spirits of smoke and flame](/wiki/spirits-of-smoke-and-flame/) and with veins of pure [quicksilver](/wiki/quicksilver/). It is fed sleeping potions and prisoners are chained to its head so they can accompany it in its dreams and distract it from its fate.
*In the [Shield of Alberon](/wiki/shield-of-alberon/) are golems made of the same impervious metal as the Shield.
*Castle Greymere ([20.00](/wiki/20-00/) is guarded by stone golems who guide visitors and strip them of their belongings.
*[Janissaries](/wiki/janissaries/) have been described as golems.
*Small golems that chirp and roll are found in the dome of the  [Alien Filcher](/wiki/alien-filcher/) ([12.27](/wiki/12-27/))
