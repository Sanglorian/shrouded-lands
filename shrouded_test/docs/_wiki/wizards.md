*Melnir was a heroic mage of old who struck the earth with his staff to summon a stream of magma that became a volcano ([51.12](/wiki/51-12/)). 
*The Witch-Queen of Cragsend was a mage. 
*Vilenius Arkhaus is an arch-mage and Wizard-Architect (also described as a magic-mason). It is unclear if other wizard-architects or magic-masons exist. 
*The mage Iraine the Suitor, who hopes to achieve immortality by marrying a god. ’s Suitor’s Tower is shrouded in storm clouds, with torture chambers where secrets as well as skin can be peeled off, labs, vats and creatures with spider limbs.
*Severard of the Seven Circles or Severard of the Seven Chins. He had a wizard tower and a number of apprentices. 
*Dormond of the Crooked Oak, apprentice to Severard. Author of the Treatise on Magics Related to Matter and Spirit. 
*Imorcar the Many is an archmage. He has a number of spellbooks his apprentices are keen to acquire. 
*Barnabus Bludenoss is a student of Severard who turned himself into a were-man to escape death. 
*Palendar, the Weeper, is an elven wizard who lives in a tall ivory tower. 
*Unwerth the Immense appears to be a wizard bachelor. 
*Taliah Bartleby is a hedge-wizardess. 
*Sala Olmsted is resident wizard of Olmsted Keep ([31.04](/wiki/31-04/)).
