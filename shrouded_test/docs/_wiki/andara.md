An ancient kingdom which was situated in the lands now covered by the [[Keening Sea]]. It is referred to by the Wedge-Stone ([[47.11]]), which reads:

: All shall be as it was in the beginning. Drowned Andara again shall see the sun; black [[Serpantia]], kingdom of worms, shall sleep beneath the waves.

: What [[Melnir]] ([[51.12]]) did shall be undone; what the Traitor Prince did shall be undone; what the Drowned await shall come to pass.

: Look to me when [[the Hammer of Bronze]] is forged anew. Look to me on the twelfth night when the sea boils. Look upon me, ye of Serpantia, and despair.

The cyclopean ruin of Monatheron ([[43.12]]) is the last remnant of Andara.
