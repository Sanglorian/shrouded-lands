*The [Dreamsong](/wiki/dreamsong/) preexisted the world. 
**It is sustained by the songs of the whales. 
**Humans in deep slumber can touch the Dreamsong, which can kill or inspire them.
**The druids of the Bitter Coast use didgeridoos to call whales to the surface to listen directly to the song ([02.07](/wiki/02-07/)).
**Elves use the mumbling of captive sleeping humans to track and record the Dreamsong ([24.07](/wiki/24-07/)).
*The [world of dreams](/wiki/world-of-dreams/) intrudes onto this one along the edges of [the Kingswood](/wiki/the-kingswood/) where hundreds of root-like tentacles reach down
**These tentacles have lenses that allow them to see the mundane world; if worn by someone in this world, they show visions of the world of dreams: grand vistas, and the things that scuttle in the corners of our eyes.
*Men who slave in [dwarven](/wiki/dwarves/) mines hear their drums in their dreams decades after they escape.
*Though most [kobolds](/wiki/kobolds/) are born of dragon's memories, some appear to be dreamed up.
**Kobolds remind humans of things half-forgotten in dreams.
**An example are those of [Celadon](/wiki/celadon/) the Shrewd ([10.12](/wiki/10-12/))
**Kobolds dream that they are dragons.
* It is said of Zaal, the Sleeping City ([08.27](/wiki/08-27/)), that here lie all dreams of beauty that men forget when they are awake.
** [Hypno](/wiki/hypno/), Lord of Sleep, lives in Zaal. He is a shepherd of humans' dreams and steals those that would inflict mental harm or plant subversive ideas.
* Elves often blame humankind's weaknesses, follies and hubris on 'renegade dreams' - those that escape [Hypno](/wiki/hypno/)
* One young lady dreamed of a firebird and woke with a firebird feather in her hand ([39.23](/wiki/39-23/))
* The Dreaming Dark is one of the [darknesses](/wiki/darknesses/) of the dwarves. It turns up strange flashes and patterns that quicken the imagination.
** Some dwarves of Barca Enmordet ([49.03](/wiki/49-03/)) have let the Dreaming Dark into their minds and are driven to create masterpieces or die trying.
* The village wise woman of the [People of the Claw](/wiki/people-of-the-claw/) is haunted nightly by strange watery dreams ([48.13](/wiki/48-13/))
* The lady of Adherion's bastard children were revealed when her lover spoke about her aloud in a dream ([30.03](/wiki/30-03/))
* Chelind dreamed about being embraced by a [being](/wiki/elemental-beings/) which led her to discover a strange artifact ([16.16](/wiki/16-16/)).
* The [Prince of Men](/wiki/prince-of-men/) dreams in Mirror Lake ([37.01](/wiki/37-01/)). His dreams bring visions of what is and what may be to the surface of the lake.
* Because of the magic [Supendar](/wiki/supendar/) worked on [Naros](/wiki/naros/), that land is now populated by nightmares.
* The Pool of Telanar can cause one to lose the ability to dream ([33.08](/wiki/33-08/)).
* The location of the High King's Crown appeared to the first [King of Gore](/wiki/royal-family-of-gore/) in a dream.
* The twelve gods of the [Undying Cycles of Creation](/wiki/undying-cycles-of-creation/) are called the Dreamers when they appear on playing cards.
** According to the Undying Cycles, the Fools are the creators of everything in existence and in dream
* [Jahur](/wiki/jahur/)'s Alleys of the Woman in Red were created by [Viceroy Duygu](/wiki/viceroy-duygu/) in an attempt to trap a figure who appeared in his dreams.
* Gnollish captives sleep alongside the Fist of Madai so they will accompany it in its dreams and keep it from waking ([34.28](/wiki/34-28/)).
