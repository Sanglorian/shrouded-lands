* *Family: **[Seutorians](/wiki/seutorians/). Count Seutorian is uncle of [Lucin Seutorian](/wiki/lucin-seutorian/) and [Cliffar Seutorian](/wiki/cliffar-seutorian/) ([11.03](/wiki/11-03/))

## Connections
* The Count funded Lucin's disastrous production of [The Song of Ban and Ulena](/wiki/the-song-of-ban-and-ulena/) ([39.12](/wiki/39-12/))

* The Count's [hot-air balloon](/wiki/balloons-(shuttered)/) looks like [Baron Harenghast](/wiki/baron-harenghast/)'s mother.
* Count Seutorian keeps a large flock of [peacockatrices](/wiki/peacockatrices/) in his gardens and often meditates while staring into their feathers (while on the other side of a stout wrought iron fence, as he is not an idiot).
* One of the Count's current guests is the orc Brigadier [Kalas Montra-brey](/wiki/kalas-montra-brey/), who Seutorian provides with assistance in his hunts in return for a promise of an elf slave of his very own, if Kalas can find one that meets his specifications.
* The Count bought the [Blind Doge](/wiki/blind-doge/) his talking pocket watch from [Albus Flidge](/wiki/albus-flidge/).
