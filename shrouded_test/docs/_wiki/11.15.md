## The Haunt of the Peryton
Connects to: [13.08](/wiki/13-08/), [17.18](/wiki/17-18/) and [29.10](/wiki/29-10/)

This stretch of hills is stalked by a [peryton](/wiki/perytons/). Perytons have the voice of and cast the shadow of the last man they have slain. Someone familiar with Severard of the Seven Circles *([13.08](/wiki/13-08/))* might startle upon hearing the peryton’s taunts.

The peryton keeps watch over a flock of deer, including a talking stag who once rode with Tehaar the Huntress herself *([29.10](/wiki/29-10/))*.
#### Connection
*A lady of **Thring** *([17.18](/wiki/17-18/))* believes that her husband was the latest man killed by the peryton, and she would pay dearly to hear his voice again.
#### Hooks:
*How did the stag make it here?
*Did the peryton truly eat Severard? Most think that one of the giant spiders killed him.
*Why did the stag leave Tehaar’s side?
