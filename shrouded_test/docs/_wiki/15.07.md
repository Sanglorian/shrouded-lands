## KFE!
![](/media/1507.png)Connects to: [05.05](/wiki/05-05/), [07.01](/wiki/07-01/), [13.08](/wiki/13-08/) and [19.31.04](/wiki/19-31/).

KFE or "Keith's southern Fried Entrails" is a local eatery that is popular among those who would enjoy tripe. [[Captain Keith]] uses he secret spice recipe and his patented "southern" style frying technique to cook some tasty entrails. What people don't know is that "southern" style frying uses the power of the mystical river rocks from the Glass Rapids ([05.05](/wiki/05-05/))!
### Connection:
*Many of the entrails come from the Tangled Web ([[13.08]]).
*A riddling giant ([[07.01]]) sometimes frequents KFE.
*The “southern” style of frying entrails comes from [[Jahur]] ([[19.31]].04).
