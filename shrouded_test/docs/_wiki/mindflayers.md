A **mindflayer** known as [Hush](/wiki/hush/) tried to redesign [Rogdag](/wiki/rogdag/)'s brain in an attempt to discover a trigger or seed for psychic ability. Instead, his experiments altered the ogre's perception of time. Rogdag could not only see future possibilities, but he also emitted a field that affected others when they were in a receptive state.
