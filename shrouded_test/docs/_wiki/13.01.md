## Overview
<p align="left" class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom:6pt;">[The Weeper](/wiki/the-weeper/)'s daughter arranged for the [Skullcrusher orcs](/wiki/skullcrusher-orcs/), then children, to be cared for in this secluded mountain valley within [the Winterjarl's marche](/wiki/the-marche-of-the-winterjarl/). They live here still and create sturdy and elegant woodwork.</p>
