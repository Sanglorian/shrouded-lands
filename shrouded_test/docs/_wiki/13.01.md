[13.01](/wiki/13-01/)

Connects to: [11.03](/wiki/11-03/), [14.00](/wiki/14-00/), [16.16.06](/wiki/16-16/) and [43.08](/wiki/43-08/).

[The Weeper](/wiki/the-weeper/)'s daughter arranged for the [Skullcrusher orcs](/wiki/skullcrusher-orcs/), then children, to be cared for in this secluded mountain valley within [the Winterjarl's marche](/wiki/the-marche-of-the-winterjarl/). They live here still and create sturdy and elegant woodwork.
