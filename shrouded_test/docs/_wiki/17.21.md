## Hill of Fist and Fang
![](/media/1721.png)Connects to: [13.24](/wiki/13-24/), [16.16](/wiki/16-16/), [16.16.05](/wiki/16-16/), [18.19](/wiki/18-19/), [18.28](/wiki/18-28/), [23.11.03](/wiki/23-11/) and the Devil’s Fingers.

This shrub-covered hill nominally marks the Duchy's extreme southern frontier. It is the site of an ancient and spectacular ritual that proves the greatest **Thringish** knights' courage and mettle, where [The Brothers of the Lion](/wiki/the-brothers-of-the-lion/) earn their lion mounts.
