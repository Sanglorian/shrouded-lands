Before countless petty quarrels drove its residents abroad, Hurlstone Hill was the center of [halfling](/wiki/halflings/) civilization. Although overgrown and crumbling, many of its once-cozy tunnels and chambers may still be explored. This has become dangerous, as the vampire [Chyndonax](/wiki/chyndonax/) is hiding here. He is attempting to evade [The Fearless](/wiki/the-fearless/), a band consisting of a sun-priest, a cleric of [Iano](/wiki/iano/), a shield maiden of [Alberon](/wiki/alberon/) and a gnome alchemist, united by their dedication to eradicating vampirism. Chyndonax has turned the bodies of the halflings murdered in ancient feuds into his servants, who stalk the grounds tirelessly, watching for intruders.

[The Bolgers](/wiki/the-bolgers/) are the only surviving halfling clan that could reclaim Hurlstone. The two other great clans, [The Grumbers](/wiki/the-grumbers/) and [The Cavengows](/wiki/the-cavengows/), faded into obscurity. The Grumbers are said to have gone east, and settled in Jar Town ([16.20](/wiki/16-20/)), where they mingled with low families until their old authority waned. The Cavengows struck out for [The Bitter Coast](/wiki/the-bitter-coast/). It is said that Hurlstone was abandoned per terms of a peace treaty between the three clans. They agreed to end, once and for all, the ancient conflicts over property and resource rights, which threatened to drench the hill in blood.

### Connections
* [The Cavengows](/wiki/the-cavengows/) are now the cloud halflings, in the north of [The Bitter Coast](/wiki/the-bitter-coast/).
