## Overview
A rocky peninsula juts into the Ocean of Bitter Regrets ([00.06](/wiki/00-06/)). At its very end is a small and twisted willow leaning well over the Ocean. At its base rests a boy with a battered straw hat tugged over his eyes. This is [Laughing Sam](/wiki/laughing-sam/).
