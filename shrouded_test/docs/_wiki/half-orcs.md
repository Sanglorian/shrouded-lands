The result of a union between [[orcs|orc]] and human.

*The [[Grimes]] are a half-orc clan. They feed human sacrifices to a Snake God.
*Brigadier Montra-brey travels with a battalion of half-orcs.
*The Pignoses all claim orcish blood. ([17.07](/wiki/17-07/)) Olga Pignose is the child of a half-orc and a half-elf, and incredibly beautiful.
*The people of Everdark claim orcish ancestry, with orc eyes and orc blood. ([01.03](/wiki/01-03/))
*Ota Verlime had a half-orc torturer ([21.06](/wiki/21-06/))
*Ambassadors to Shuttered from [[Grumluda]] tend to be half-orcs. They are granted special dispensation to enter the city without a *geas*, but it is unclear if that is because they are half-human or because they are ambassadors.
