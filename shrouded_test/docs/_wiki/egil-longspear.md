**Family:** [[Longspears]]. [[Hardrald Longspear]] is his father.

[[Egil Longspear]] ([[23.11]].03) was [[Hardrald Longspear|Hardrald]]'s youngest son. He was too young to remember his father's sacrifice, but this did nothing to deflate his ego and ambition as he became a mercenary. On the behest of Newhill ([[17.07]]), he led one of the few successful human incursions into the [[Kingswood]]. He returned with more than a dozen human children [[Cerelaine]] had kidnapped. For this act, the elf holds a grudge against his descendants to this day.
