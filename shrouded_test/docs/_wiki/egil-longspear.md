* *Family:** [Longspears](/wiki/longspears/). [Hardrald Longspear](/wiki/hardrald-longspear/) is his father.

[Egil Longspear](/wiki/egil-longspear/) ([23.11](/wiki/23-11/).03) was [Hardrald](/wiki/hardrald-longspear/)'s youngest son. He was too young to remember his father's sacrifice, but this did nothing to deflate his ego and ambition as he became a mercenary. On the behest of Newhill ([17.07](/wiki/17-07/)), he led one of the few successful human incursions into the [Kingswood](/wiki/kingswood/). He returned with more than a dozen human children [Cerelaine](/wiki/cerelaine/) had kidnapped. For this act, the elf holds a grudge against his descendants to this day.
