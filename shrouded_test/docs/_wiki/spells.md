A **cantrip** is a simple spell. In Shuttered, they teach the [[Thousand Spells and One]], although only in the [[High Tongue]]. Most Shuttered nobles are capable of producing a few cantrips. 

*[[Jackalweres|Jackalwere]] mages ([[51.17]]) cast a hex that makes its victims incapable of speaking or writing in anything other than gibberish. 
*The [[Spellknight]] is the only one who can summon the Phantom Steed. He has polymorphed [[Chicken dragons|chickens into dragons]]. 
*Wizards casting *fireball* was mentioned ([[Temple of the Labyrinth]]).
*[[Bogarus Bolger]], no wizard, has a pin enchanted with a spell, *[[Bogarus' safe escape|Bogarus’ safe escape]],* that when triggered teleports the caster 1d100 x 10 feet in a random direction. 
*[[Melda the Boneless]] casts *the unstoppable thrust* by contorting her body into a glyph. 
*The [[Skibart halflings]] have many stones with *light* cast on them. 
*The *sleep* spell interacts interestingly with a [[Winged cats|cat feather]] pillow.
**Summon monster* spells are a favourite of [[Tehaar the Huntress|Tehaar]]’s band. 
**Identify* spells have a material component - a 100 gp pearl. 
*A *darkness* spell is triggered in [[13.08]] when a chest is opened. 
*The [[ostrlich]] was created because a powerful spell to resurrect someone after death was applied to the wrong entity. 
*The *[[ever-winding worm]]* is a spell, the material component of which is the most perfectly-formed worm to be found within a thirteen-day-old corpse. The worm delivers a very slow death. 
*[[Severard's Spellbook|Severard's spellbook]] includes *Severard’s ferrous imperative*, a ray that draws all magnetic things to the caster; and *piquant premonition,* which is cast over a meal as the caster cooks it. The mental reaction of those who eat it is then known to the caster. 
*The [[Whispered Caress]] is a divine spell that causes the caster to grow shadowy, terrifying tentacles.
