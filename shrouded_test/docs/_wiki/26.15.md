## Overview
Here stands the Kennel, the great fortress-monastery-cum-factory-cum-prison-cum-mine of the [Canes Sanguis](/wiki/canes-sanguis/). Here initiates are broken in, and heretics toil to death and beyond in eternal repentance by pulling metals and gems from the mines, and working the factories, all to fill the coffers and vaults that finance the well-armed Order.
