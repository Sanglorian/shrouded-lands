Wormroot warms the blood.

Originally called 'wyrmroot' and associated with dragons and dragonfire. 'Greater Wormroot' is found in the Mouth of the Dragon ([[10.01]]). It is supposedly more potent than the surface-grown variety. The [[Zoar Rajak]] goblins call Greater Wormroot 'dragon's plant' and will try to stop others from gathering it; it features as an evil force in their myths.

### Connections
*Found in [[32.32]] around the sinkhole that begins the [[ley lines|elemental line of fire]].
*The narrator of [[With Morning Comes Mistfall]] describes gathering wormroot.
*Turning into human women required the bears in [[13.17]] to eat wormroot.
*In the Farnsfall Holding ([[11.03]]), [[Cliffar Seutorian]] hopes to farm and export wormroot.
