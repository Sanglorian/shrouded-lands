Wormroot warms the blood.

Originally called 'wyrmroot' and associated with dragons and dragonfire. 'Greater Wormroot' is found in the Mouth of the Dragon ([10.01](/wiki/10-01/)). It is supposedly more potent than the surface-grown variety. The [Zoar Rajak](/wiki/zoar-rajak/) goblins call Greater Wormroot 'dragon's plant' and will try to stop others from gathering it; it features as an evil force in their myths.

### Connections
* Found in [32.32](/wiki/32-32/) around the sinkhole that begins the [elemental line of fire](/wiki/ley-lines/).
* The narrator of [With Morning Comes Mistfall](/wiki/with-morning-comes-mistfall/) describes gathering wormroot.
* Turning into human women required the bears in [13.17](/wiki/13-17/) to eat wormroot.
* In the Farnsfall Holding ([11.03](/wiki/11-03/)), [Cliffar Seutorian](/wiki/cliffar-seutorian/) hopes to farm and export wormroot.
