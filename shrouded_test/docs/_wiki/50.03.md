## Overview
<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom:6.0pt;mso-para-margin-bottom:.5gd"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:9.0pt;font-family:"CenturyGothic","sans-serif"; color:black;mso-themecolor:text1">Far out into the nightmare jungles beyond the World's Edge, there lies an enormous pile of reeking detritus - offal, feces and bones. This is the ancestral defecating-ground of the [hoarlephants](/wiki/the-world's-edge-and-beyond/), which are among the most vile and terrible of all the World's Edge monsters. </span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom:6.0pt;mso-para-margin-bottom:.5gd">More than a hundred years ago now, the Thringish paladin Sir Hyfres descended the World's Edge on the back of the famed charger [Garendar](/wiki/garendar/) ([16.16.04](/wiki/16-16/)). Before he could complete his quest, both he and the horse were devoured by a particularly noxious bull hoarlephant, and their bones were subsequently left in the bone heap. So, to this day, it is sometimes possible to see treasure hunters and knights climbing over mountains of filthy bone and stinking garbage, in search of the bones of Garendar the Brave.</p>
