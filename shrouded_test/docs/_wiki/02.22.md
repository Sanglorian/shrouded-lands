## The Castle of the Collector
Connects to: [16.17](/wiki/16-17/), [38.28](/wiki/38-28/), [45.24](/wiki/45-24/) and [50.03](/wiki/50-03/).

[[The Collector]] keeps his collections here, including his one constant collection, the first collection, which is the Hall of Skulls. In this hall, thrice expanded by masons since its original construction, there are a total of seventy-nine [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]] skulls. Some are small enough to lift in one hand, while others are enormous enough that whole banquets can be held inside them. Many of these have come from Thring, whose knights have several times managed to behead the immortal beast.

### Connections:
*One of the largest skulls yet to be collected is sunk in the [[River Hyad]] (at [[45.24]]).

*The [[Tender Hunt]] occurs here.
