A type of eel.

* There are electric eels in Dragon Lake ([25.16](/wiki/25-16/))

* Electric eels are a key part of [Winds](/wiki/winds/) governance system, as an eelmistress uses trained electric eels to zap councillors if they lie or are hostile.
