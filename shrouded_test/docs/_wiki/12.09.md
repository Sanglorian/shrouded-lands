## Canopy of the Azimyth Bats
Connects to: [02.05](/wiki/02-05/), [11.08](/wiki/11-08/), [13.08](/wiki/13-08/), [13.09](/wiki/13-09/), [15.18](/wiki/15-18/), [46.06.01](/wiki/46-06/) and the Kingswood

The broad-leaf forest grows dense here, the ancient branches intertwining so that scarcely a ray of light shines through. Out of the corner of the eye, shapes seem to move in the dark canopy above, and only the keenest of eyes finds the giant bats hanging upside on perches. These are no ordinary bats, however. These are the [azimyth](/wiki/azimyth/).
