## The Foolish Sages of Border’s Hill
![](/media/2018.png)Connects to: 13.12, 16.16, 16.16.05, 16.23, 18.26, 20.20, 22.16 and 46.01.

Long ago, a watchtower was built atop this hill, where men-at-arms would guard the borders of **Thring** against incursions. Nowadays the tower is mostly in ruins, and the only inhabitants are a conclave of eccentric sages, [[The Foolish Sages]] (or the Hunters of Truth, as they call themselves).
