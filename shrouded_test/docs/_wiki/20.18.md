Long ago, a watchtower was built atop this hill, where men-at-arms would guard the borders of **Thring** against incursions. Nowadays the tower is mostly in ruins, and the only inhabitants are a conclave of eccentric sages, [The Foolish Sages](/wiki/the-foolish-sages/) (or the Hunters of Truth, as they call themselves).
