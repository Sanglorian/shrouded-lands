## The Wreck of the Fat Crow
Region: the Lost Colony.

Connects to: [13.09](/wiki/13-09/), [48.13](/wiki/48-13/), [48.18](/wiki/48-18/), [51.12](/wiki/51-12/) and [51.17](/wiki/51-17/).

## Overview
<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom:6.0pt;mso-para-margin-bottom:.5gd"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:9.0pt;font-family:"CenturyGothic","sans-serif"; mso-bidi-font-family:"TimesNewRoman";color:black;mso-themecolor:text1">The Fat Crow, a battered two-masted merchantman, ran aground on this deserted coast after the [People of the Claw](/wiki/people-of-the-claw/) ([48.13](/wiki/48-13/)) managed to set fire to its sails. Its cargo hold contains, among more mundane things, the honey of [giant bees](/wiki/giant-bees/) ([13.09](/wiki/13-09/)). [Baron Pignose](/wiki/baron-pignose/) of Sosaria ([48.18](/wiki/48-18/)) ordered barrels of the honey to cure beef. The jerky made from the process is not only delicious, but acts as a *cure light wounds* potion.</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom:6.0pt;mso-para-margin-bottom:.5gd"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:9.0pt;font-family:"CenturyGothic","sans-serif"; mso-bidi-font-family:"TimesNewRoman";color:black;mso-themecolor:text1">Right now, it is uncertain whether or not the honey will reach Sosaria. The captain was eaten by [jackalweres](/wiki/jackalweres/) ([51.17](/wiki/51-17/)) and the first mate, [Charlotte "Greedy Paw" Pond](/wiki/charlotte-pond/), is considering absconding with the honey and selling it to old friends from her pirate days. The remnants of the crew guard the ship. They have learned that jackalweres can imitate humans, and they are suspicious of anyone who approaches. Their supplies are dwindling, and they might warm up to someone offering water or rations.</span></p>
