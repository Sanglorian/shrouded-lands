* *Region:** [The Hills of Gore](/wiki/the-hills-of-gore/)
Connects to: [07.17](/wiki/07-17/), [08.01](/wiki/08-01/), [29.14.47](/wiki/29-14/).

The people of Gore hold their coming of age rituals in this lush glade. Here, boys become men, girls become women and girls become men. In this glade, [the Daughter of Ser Gareth Lemarr](/wiki/the-daughter-of-ser-gareth-lemarr/) cut her hair short with her father’s razor-like sword. Such is the way of the people of Gore: that girls will be girls and boys will be boys, but a woman may be a man whenever she needs.
### Connection
* [Elharen the Silent](/wiki/elharen-the-silent/) ([29.14.47](/wiki/29-14/)) has come here to seek aid in her personal war against the gnolls of the Burning Lands.
### Hooks
* Can men be women whenever they need?
* What does it mean for a maid of Gore to be a man? Is it just a change in behaviour and dress?
