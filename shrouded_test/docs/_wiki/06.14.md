Here, at the edge of the wood where the dryads ([05.14](/wiki/05-14/)) now live is a great arch of sod held up over the ground by poles. Although the poles lean drunkenly and most of the grass has now died, brown stains of dried blood can be found by careful investigation under the arch. Obviously, this is the side where the ritual by which blood brothers are made was performed.
