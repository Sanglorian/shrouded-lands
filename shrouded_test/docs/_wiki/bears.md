## Types
* The [Lochgates](/wiki/lochgates/) are a clan of [werebears](/wiki/werebears/).
* The [nordanbjorn](/wiki/nordanbjorn/) are talking bears, ruled by the [bjornajarl](/wiki/bjornajarl/) [Thorek Ironhide](/wiki/thorek-ironhide/). Their homeland is the [Marche of the Winterjarl](/wiki/marche-of-the-winterjarl/).
* [Drop bears](/wiki/drop-bears/)
* The knights of Castle Karandur ([13.17](/wiki/13-17/)) ride bears. The Lady of Karandur traces her line back to a bear who asked the Pacharia to be transformed into a human woman. She displays a bearskin above her hearth which she claims was her ancestor's first skin.
* Cave bears

## Bears
* [One-Eyed Kevanjr](/wiki/one-eyed-kevanjr/) is an old bear-bard who can recite in their entirety seven sagas of seven days' duration each. He defeated [Arinlag Fivefish](/wiki/arinlag-fivefish/) in a fight for [Karsta Thoreksdottir](/wiki/karsta-thoreksdottir/)'s love.
* [Arinlag Fivefish](/wiki/arinlag-fivefish/) is a bear-bard who lives in the Kingswood.
* [Thorek Ironhide](/wiki/thorek-ironhide/) is bjornajarl of the nordanbjorn
* [Karsta Thoreksdottir](/wiki/karsta-thoreksdottir/) or [Karsta the Slender](/wiki/karsta-the-slender/) is favourite daughter of Thorek. She has a child, which Arinlag and Kevanjr both claim as their own.
* The previous jarl's body is frozen in ice and some hope that she is still alive ([14.01](/wiki/14-01/))
* The [Lochgates](/wiki/lochgates/), including [Sweitbor the Roarer](/wiki/sweitbor-the-roarer/), the fifth duke of Thring

## Connections
* Dwarven monastics smear bear fat on their faces when they go on pilgrimage to Mount Scorshia ([02.03](/wiki/02-03/)).
* [Grimvlech the Smiler](/wiki/grimvlech-the-smiler/) brought down a bear with nothing but his silver teeth. He was offered marriage to an Anghart heir in the hope that he would defend against the [Lochgates](/wiki/lochgates/).
* Some [Scarecrow](/wiki/scarecrows/) nests are build in abandoned bear's dens.
* The king of Hound's Hearth, [Hjalk](/wiki/hjalk/), has an ailing fire-breathing bear, [Snalfi](/wiki/snalfi/).
* A patch of land against the south wall of the [Shuttered City](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/) belongs to the [Grumluda embassy](/wiki/embassies-shuttered/) since the unfortunate incident of Doge Montisse and the twenty-six shaven cave bears
