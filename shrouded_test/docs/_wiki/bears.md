## Types
*The [[Lochgates]] are a clan of [[werebears]].
*The [[nordanbjorn]] are talking bears, ruled by the [[bjornajarl]] [[Thorek Ironhide]]. Their homeland is the [[Marche of the Winterjarl]]. 
*[[Drop bears]]
*The knights of Castle Karandur ([[13.17]]) ride bears. The Lady of Karandur traces her line back to a bear who asked the Pacharia to be transformed into a human woman. She displays a bearskin above her hearth which she claims was her ancestor's first skin.
*Cave bears
## Bears
*[[One-Eyed Kevanjr]] is an old bear-bard who can recite in their entirety seven sagas of seven days' duration each. He defeated [[Arinlag Fivefish]] in a fight for [[Karsta Thoreksdottir]]'s love.
*[[Arinlag Fivefish]] is a bear-bard who lives in the Kingswood.
*[[Thorek Ironhide]] is bjornajarl of the nordanbjorn
*[[Karsta Thoreksdottir]] or [[Karsta the Slender]] is favourite daughter of Thorek. She has a child, which Arinlag and Kevanjr both claim as their own.
*The previous jarl's body is frozen in ice and some hope that she is still alive ([[14.01]])
*The [[Lochgates]], including [[Sweitbor the Roarer]], the fifth duke of Thring
## Connections
*Dwarven monastics smear bear fat on their faces when they go on pilgrimage to Mount Scorshia ([[02.03]]).
*[[Grimvlech the Smiler]] brought down a bear with nothing but his silver teeth. He was offered marriage to an Anghart heir in the hope that he would defend against the [[Lochgates]].
*Some [[Scarecrows|Scarecrow]] nests are build in abandoned bear's dens.
*The king of Hound's Hearth, [[Hjalk]], has an ailing fire-breathing bear, [[Snalfi]]. 
*A patch of land against the south wall of the [[Shuttered City]] belongs to the [[embassies (Shuttered)|Grumluda embassy]] since the unfortunate incident of Doge Montisse and the twenty-six shaven cave bears
