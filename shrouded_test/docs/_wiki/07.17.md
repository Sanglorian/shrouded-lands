## Castle Maratan
![](/media/SL_gore5.png)Connects to: [11.15](/wiki/11-15/), [16.04](/wiki/16-04/), [16.16.01](/wiki/16-16/), [38.28](/wiki/38-28/) and the Duchy of Thring.

In an island in a lake rises the squat wooden bulk of Castle Maratan. If can only be approached by a narrow bridge that crosses the lake waters, which makes it difficult to besiege. The waters of the lake are stagnant, otherwise the castle would've been abandoned long ago as the [Lords Sanguine](/wiki/lords-sanguine/) cannot bear to look upon running water.

The current ruler of Castle Maratan is [Lady Natala](/wiki/lady-natala/). Her knights are the [snickersnees](/wiki/snickersnees/).
