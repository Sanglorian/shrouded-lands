[Lady Delasar](/wiki/lady-delasar/) conjured up a tribe of **homunculi** to keep her brother company in the old Delasar mansion ([08.03](/wiki/08-03/)). They are half-man and half-beetle, created in the beetle badlands ([06.05](/wiki/06-05/)). Each homonculus is roughly three feet tall, vaguely insectoid, and vicious when provoked. Ogwyn has even taken a she-beetle to be his wife. Part of the Delasar's sumptous vegetable crop goes to feeding this population.

The beetle-men will fight to defend the mansion and their master.

* [Beetlefolk](/wiki/beetlefolk/) similar to these homunculi live far to the east ([49.03](/wiki/49-03/)).
