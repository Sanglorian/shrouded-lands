## Overview
[The Hoardmasters](/wiki/hoard/) are not happy. It has been a month since the last news from Station Seven. Yesterday, a wild eyed dwarf was found at Titan's Rest ([33.01](/wiki/33-01/)). She—for in her state, her sex was shamefully obvious—talked of a sinister presence hunting the miners through the station: [the Haunting Dark](/wiki/darknesses/). 

The dwarves know that there are many darknesses. But it is the Haunting Dark that was summoned to these tunnels by the curse of a dying dwarf. And it cannot be satiated.
