## Overview
[[Hoard|The Hoardmasters]] are not happy. It has been a month since the last news from Station Seven. Yesterday, a wild eyed dwarf was found at Titan's Rest ([[33.01]]). She—for in her state, her sex was shamefully obvious—talked of a sinister presence hunting the miners through the station: [[Darknesses|the Haunting Dark]]. 

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom:6.0pt;mso-para-margin-bottom:.5gd"><span lang="EN-US" style="font-size:9.0pt;font-family:"CenturyGothic","sans-serif"; color:black;mso-themecolor:text1">The dwarves know that there are many darknesses. </span>But it is the Haunting Dark that was summoned to these tunnels by the curse of a dying dwarf. And it cannot be satiated. </p>
