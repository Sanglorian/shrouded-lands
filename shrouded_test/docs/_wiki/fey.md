**|thumb|400px]]Fey**, **fairies** or **faeries**, dwell in the [Fey-Realm](/wiki/fey-realm/) and have a relationship with the elves. The [Bound Bargainers](/wiki/bound-bargainers/) (a faction of the elves) make gifts to and contracts with the fey; even the [Bloodied King](/wiki/bloodied-king/) has contracts he must keep (including an annual delivery of human babies).

The Holt has many doors because of a clever agreement made by an elfmaid with a prince of the fey.
