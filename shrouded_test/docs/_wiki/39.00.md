## Overview
The priests of this shrine to the Speaker of Bronze ([33.01](/wiki/33-01/)) believe that their patron can be returned to life if dwarves keep faith in him-her. They pray in shifts, so that every second for the last 999 years prayers to the titan have rung out.
Recently, priests have spoken in tongues. One has prophesized that the Speaker to Bronze will be reborn as a dwarf-child to parents who have never touched iron.

## Connections
-Such a dwarf-child is more likely to be born among the iron-poor dwarves of the Burning Lands ([31.27](/wiki/31-27/)) than elsewhere.
-But perhaps that dwarf has already been born to the west of here ([14.02](/wiki/14-02/)).

## Hooks
-What does the Hoard ([33.00](/wiki/33-00/)) think of the shrine?

-What do the priests of the Drinker of Iron think of the shrine?
