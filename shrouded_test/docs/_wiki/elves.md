## Deities
The [[Green Lady]] is said to be the mother of the elves.
## Peoples
The elves are divided between the [[seelie elves|seelie]] (Vo Tume in their own language) and [[unseelie elves|unseelie]].

Most unseelie went into exile with the [[Queen Sinister]]; most seelie remained. The [[white elves]] of [[Zaal]] are exiled seelie; [[the Drow]] and the Chief Librarian ([[28.07]]) are unseelie who remained.

The [[quicklings]] are also an elfish people, though whether they are seelie, unseelie, neither or both is yet to be specified in a hex entry.

Likewise, the [[high elves]] of [[Alacoda]], a distant empire, are of unknown stock.
## Seelie Accords
The elves allowed [[The Prince of Men]] to log their forests if he became a signatory of the Seelie Accords, which shared out elfish lands between seelie and unseelie, and returned the Seal of the One King from Mirror Lake ([[37.01]]).
