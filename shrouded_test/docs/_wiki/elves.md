## Deities
The [Green Lady](/wiki/green-lady/) is said to be the mother of the elves.
## Peoples
The elves are divided between the [seelie](/wiki/seelie-elves/) (Vo Tume in their own language) and [unseelie](/wiki/unseelie-elves/).

Most unseelie went into exile with the [Queen Sinister](/wiki/queen-sinister/); most seelie remained. The [white elves](/wiki/white-elves/) of [Zaal](/wiki/zaal/) are exiled seelie; [the Drow](/wiki/the-drow/) and the Chief Librarian ([28.07](/wiki/28-07/)) are unseelie who remained.

The [quicklings](/wiki/quicklings/) are also an elfish people, though whether they are seelie, unseelie, neither or both is yet to be specified in a hex entry.

Likewise, the [high elves](/wiki/high-elves/) of [Alacoda](/wiki/alacoda/), a distant empire, are of unknown stock.
## Seelie Accords
The elves allowed [The Prince of Men](/wiki/the-prince-of-men/) to log their forests if he became a signatory of the Seelie Accords, which shared out elfish lands between seelie and unseelie, and returned the Seal of the One King from Mirror Lake ([37.01](/wiki/37-01/)).
