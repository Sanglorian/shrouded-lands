The gharsang are the wooden idols depicting the lords of the sky and the lords of the underworld that are venerated by the [orcs](/wiki/orcs/). For two different takes on the lords of the sky, see [27.01](/wiki/27-01/) and [25.01](/wiki/25-01/).

The lords of the sky apparently give substantial boons to the orc race, including a rain of gold. The lords of the sky and of the underworld are represented as gharsang - hideous leering faces carved from wood. The orcs believe that the idols scare off evil spirits, strip away illusions and guard orcish lands against enchantment. [Gruumsch](/wiki/gruumsch/) is chief of the lords of the underworld.
