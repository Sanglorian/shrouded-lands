Also known as **small gods** or, to the [Temple Indivisible](/wiki/temple-indivisible/), **preternatural agents**.

The [Street of Small Gods](/wiki/street-of-small-gods/) is a collection of Alberon's alleged lovers, progeny, martyrs and so on. 

The Fugitive Gods ([36.09](/wiki/36-09/)) are statuettes saved at the end of the [Time of Schisms](/wiki/time-of-schisms/) when the [Temple Indivisible](/wiki/temple-indivisible/) destroyed all the little gods. It's not clear why the Temple destroyed the little gods but tolerates the Street of Small Gods; perhaps its position has softened, or perhaps it was their representation in statuette form that the Temple found intolerable.
