Also known as '''small gods''' or, to the [[Temple Indivisible]], '''preternatural agents'''.

The [[Street of Small Gods]] is a collection of Alberon's alleged lovers, progeny, martyrs and so on. 

The Fugitive Gods ([[36.09]]) are statuettes saved at the end of the [[Time of Schisms]] when the [[Temple Indivisible]] destroyed all the little gods. It's not clear why the Temple destroyed the little gods but tolerates the Street of Small Gods; perhaps its position has softened, or perhaps it was their representation in statuette form that the Temple found intolerable.
