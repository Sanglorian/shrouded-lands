Among the orcs of the Grey Mountains, women are entitled to fill any male role provided they also fulfill every duty of a woman. Stories are told of legendary orc soldiers fighting with their whelps strapped to their back. Some orc matrons will even marry other women, acting as provider and protector for them.
### Connection:
*One way of avoiding the Double Duty is to bully a human into raising your child ([[22.03]]).
