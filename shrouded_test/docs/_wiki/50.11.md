## Overview
Creatures of extraordinary beauty known as [birds of paradise](/wiki/birds-of-paradise/) flit through this stretch of jungle's canopy. During mating season, birdwatchers can view their mating rituals from the World's Edge. The rituals are sophisticated, and sometimes compared to [Shuttered](/wiki/shuttered/)'s [Shadow Ballet](/wiki/shadow-ballet/) and the [Two-Faced Dancers](/wiki/two-faced-dancers/) of Jahur in terms of artistry.
