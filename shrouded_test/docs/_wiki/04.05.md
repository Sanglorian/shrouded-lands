[04.05](/wiki/04-05/)

**Region: **[The Westmarches](/wiki/the-westmarches/)

High in the mountains, a series of hot springs feed into a steaming pool where the beautiful and elusive firebirds fly from their distant nesting grounds to splash and cavort. In a series of mist-shrouded waterfalls, the overflowing waters of the pool flow down to the valley floor far below.

Many have sought to brave the dangers of the mountains to capture a living firebird, but the cliffs below the Pool have proven steep and the perils many…

* The dwarves of a nearby monastery ([03.04](/wiki/03-04/)) frequent the Pool and collect orange stones from it.

* [Father Dorek](/wiki/father-dorek/) (a wandering dwarf who maintains a small shrine ([03.07](/wiki/03-07/)) is also interested in this pool for reasons of his own.
* This is the source of the [Glass Rapids](/wiki/glass-rapids/)
