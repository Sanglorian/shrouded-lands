One of the [[Lords Sanguine]]

**Rules:** Castle Ravenscraig ([[03.19]]).

Lady Belal of the Nets is slowly rebuilding her ancestral castle in the midst of the city's ruins. She spends most of her days with her ladies-in-waiting, weaving fishing nets from the silk of the giant spiders that dwell in the crumbling undercity. She hopes to build nets mighty enough to snare the great whales that frolic offshore. She finds her ambitions constantly sabotaged by other [[Lords Sanguine]] who fear that [[The Pirate Kings]] will return if she begins to accumulate wealth.
