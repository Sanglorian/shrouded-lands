## The Lornfields
![](/media/1411.png)Connects to: [13.10](/wiki/13-10/), [15.11](/wiki/15-11/) and [22.10](/wiki/22-10/).

Despite their name, the Lornfields mostly contains forested hills. It has not been inhabited for quite some time due to the local humans being wary of the [werebears](/wiki/werebears/) that live nearby ([13.10](/wiki/13-10/)) but old stone walls and foundation pits can still be found among the tres.

Since trees have been allowed to regrow across this area, the Lornfields have grown eerily quiet with few deer and fewer birds. [Lady Anghart](/wiki/lady-anghart/) and her "fiance" ([15.11](/wiki/15-11/)) plan to resettle the area after coming to an agreement with the bears and giving them a thoughtful gift of a drinking tankard made out of [Grimvlech the Smiler](/wiki/grimvlech-the-smiler/)'s skull. Anghart hopes that if she offers generous enough terms she'll be able to round up at least a few dozen peasant families after her wedding.

What the Lady does not know is that some time ago a [demon](/wiki/demons/) fell from the blackness behind the stars ([22.10](/wiki/22-10/)) into a hidden tar pit and remains there still, its dark thoughts lashing out from its prison.
