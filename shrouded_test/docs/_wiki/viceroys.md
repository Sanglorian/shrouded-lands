![A Viceroy](/media/Gs74.jpg)
[Viceroy Baltas](/wiki/viceroy-baltas/) turns a blind eye to his concubine [Isane the Beauty](/wiki/isane-the-beauty/); the other Viceroys suspect her of associating with the heretical [Holy Fools](/wiki/holy-fools/).

[Viceroy Orhan](/wiki/viceroy-orhan/) was attacked in his bed last month, which has set the town on edge. He is an alchemist who made his own wives.

[Viceroy Rullaj](/wiki/viceroy-rullaj/) has a daughter, [Helin il-Helan](/wiki/helin-il-helan/), whom Jahuris believe to be caged in the [Sublime Divan](/wiki/sublime-divan/).

The Viceroys meet in the [Sublime Divan](/wiki/sublime-divan/).
