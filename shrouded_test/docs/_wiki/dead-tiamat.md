# History
# Features
* The descendants of those reshaped by Tiamat's blood are the dragonblooded.
* [Bone shards](/wiki/bone-shards/) chewed by Tiamat are a ritual currency.

# Heads of Tiamat
* According to rumour, the foundations of [Skullreach](/wiki/skullreach/) are the skull of Tiamat's blue head.
* Likewise, some say the heat of the [Burning Lands](/wiki/burning-lands/) is due to the red dragon's skull - perhaps Tiamat's - at the heart of Blackhorn's Maze ([39.32](/wiki/39-32/)).
* Tiamat's black head fell on what is now the Stolen Hills ([24.11](/wiki/24-11/)), corrupting the land. The head is no longer there; what happened to it is disputed.
* A merchant in the [Goblin Markets](/wiki/goblin-markets/) claims to have Tiamat's shrunken white head in a sack.

# Worshippers
* Kolfrosta, in The Temple of the Dead God ([03.13](/wiki/03-13/))
* Tiamat Reborn and his followers attempt to achieve his apotheosis as he believes himself to be a reincarnation of Tiamat. They occupy the Castle with Five Gates ([26.16.02](/wiki/26-16/)).
* A band of cultists have established themselves in some caves in the Grey Mountains ([14.02](/wiki/14-02/)).
* The [Tiamatan Revival](/wiki/tiamatan-revival/) is another group that reveres Tiamat. Splitters!
* John Luciferus, a mad lictor of the Tiamat Cult, wrote the [Book of Not Being Boiled in Fire](/wiki/book-of-not-being-boiled-in-fire/) before being burned at the stake.
* A cult in Cragsend ([04.06](/wiki/04-06/)) is led by a cleric-turned-sorcerer who worships a dragon, but not Tiamat.
