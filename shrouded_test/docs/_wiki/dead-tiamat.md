==History==
==Features==
*The descendants of those reshaped by Tiamat's blood are the [[dragonblooded]].
*[[Bone shards]] chewed by Tiamat are a [[ritual currencies|ritual currency]].
==Heads of Tiamat==
*According to rumour, the foundations of [[Skullreach]] are the skull of Tiamat's blue head.
*Likewise, some say the heat of the [[Burning Lands]] is due to the red dragon's skull - perhaps Tiamat's - at the heart of Blackhorn's Maze ([[39.32]]).
*Tiamat's black head fell on what is now the Stolen Hills ([[24.11]]), corrupting the land. The head is no longer there; what happened to it is disputed.
*A merchant in the [[Goblin Markets]] claims to have Tiamat's shrunken white head in a sack.

==Worshippers==

*Kolfrosta, in The Temple of the Dead God ([[03.13]])
*Tiamat Reborn and his followers attempt to achieve his apotheosis as he believes himself to be a reincarnation of Tiamat. They occupy the Castle with Five Gates ([[26.16]].02).
*A band of cultists have established themselves in some caves in the Grey Mountains ([[14.02]]).
*The [[Tiamatan Revival]] is another group that reveres Tiamat. Splitters!
*[[John Luciferus]], a mad lictor of the Tiamat Cult, wrote the [[Book of Not Being Boiled in Fire]] before being burned at the stake.
*A cult in Cragsend ([[04.06]]) is led by a cleric-turned-sorcerer who worships a dragon, but not Tiamat.
