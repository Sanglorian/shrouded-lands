## The Misplaced Obelisk

Connects to: [19.31](/wiki/19-31/), [20.18](/wiki/20-18/), [23.32](/wiki/23-32/), [33.00](/wiki/33-00/), [46.00](/wiki/46-00/) and [49.03](/wiki/49-03/).

## Overview
In the central park of Jahar ([19.31](/wiki/19-31/)) is a chain of dark marble obelisks, each indistinguishable from the one three paces before and the one three paces after it. Towards the end of the chain is a gap of six paces.

Half an obelisk of identical size and make, though toppled on its side, has been unearthed on a cliff overlooking the Maw ([46.00](/wiki/46-00/)) by a band of Hoard ([33.00](/wiki/33-00/)) dwarves who reported the discovery to the Foolish Sages ([20.18](/wiki/20-18/)) but otherwise refused to explain their business by the Maw.

Simple farmers who live near the obelisk report that by night strange tall figures - half again the size of a man - appear around the obelisk. They shift dirt and stone, reshaping the landscape for their own inscrutable purposes. Whether they are violent is unknown; recently some members of the town have gone missing.![The dark figure is a Man of the End.](/media/The_Efficiency_Expert.jpg)
The children have recently learned or invented a nursery rhyme that warns against making eye contact with the ‘Men of the End,’ because when it is broken they swallow you up and carry you in their bellies to their homes. The children will not comment on who introduced them to the rhyme.
