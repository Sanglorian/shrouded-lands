The Pignoses are a [[Newhill]] family that proudly claim some [[orcs|orcish blood]]. They are physically brutish but fairly gentle. They are often in conflict with the [[Verlimes]] but peaceful towards the [[Stannevs]].
## Pignoses
*Big Sal Pignose is the most accomplished Pignose in Newhill. She's a successful trader, enough so that she purchased a citizen's charter from the [[City of Shuttered Windows]]. Her kids are spoiled brats and often get into brawls with the younger of the [[Verlimes]].
*Baron [[Horvath Pignose]] is a wayward scion of the clan. He leads the White faction of [[Cattle barons (Sosaria)|Sosaria's cattle barons]].
*[[Olga Pignose]] is the child of [[Hilda Pignose]], a half-orc, and a travelling half-elf bard. She is extraordinarily beautiful, tall and graceful, and is connected to a prophesy concerning [[Alberon]]. She is also, inexplicably, winged.
*[[Rugose Pignose]] begs for death while he is slowly devoured by cacti mold in the [[Forest of Abominations]].
