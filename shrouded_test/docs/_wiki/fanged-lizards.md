**Fanged lizards** or **fangbeasts** are used as mounts by the outriders of [lizardman](/wiki/lizardmen/) caravans. When knights attacked Smuggler's Isle ([17.16](/wiki/17-16/), the fanged lizards ripped them to shreds.
