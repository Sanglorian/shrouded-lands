![](/media/481802.png)The Palace of All Time will probably always be the highest building in [Sosaria](/wiki/sosaria/), but it is dwarfed in sheer size by its neighbor, the Trunk. The Trunk is Sosaria's vast slaughterhouse. It is terrible to behold - a winding, sprawling earthen mound that leaks offal, blood and viscera into the Sweet Bay. The city's [cattle barons](/wiki/cattle-barosn-sosaria/) are joint shareholders in the Trunk's operation. Each employs an army of butchers, jerky makers, tanners, cleaners and plague doctors to keep the city's industry rolling.
