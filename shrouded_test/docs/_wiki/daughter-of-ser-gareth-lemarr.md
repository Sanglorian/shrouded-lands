It is the fourteenth birthday of the daughter of Ser Gareth Lemarr, her coming of age. She has not seen him since she was four. He was a brave snickersnee of [Lady Natala](/wiki/lady-natala/) and she has used his razor-like sword to cut her hair short in the Glade of Womanhood ([07.18](/wiki/07-18/))

Gareth’s shield was entirely red (see [Snickersnees](/wiki/snickersnees/)), but it was lost alongside him.

The circumstances of Ser Gareth’s death are unclear, but include a brutish Thringish knight called [Sir Mimslet](/wiki/sir-mimslet/). He is known by his harlequin chequer and kite shield. The daughter of Gareth plans to become the man’s squire and discover more about the death of her father.
