The Basilisk dwells at the top of the Pilgrim's Spire ([[25.31]]), turning mendicants to stone.

Seven dancers of the [[Shadow Ballet]] in the [[Grey Comedy]] are allegedly [[firebirds]] turned to stone by the Basilisk and then revived by [[Rosetta]].

The author of the [[Unifying Theory of Blood]] speculates that the Basilisk or [[Medusa]] may be infected by elemental earth, and would turn to air in the sunlight.
