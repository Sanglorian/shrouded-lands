## The Dune Walker
Region: [[The Singing Wastes]]

## Overview
In this stretch of the Singing Wastes, ghouls, snakes, and even scorpions are scarce. The only singing a traveler is likely to hear is the whistle of wind against the sand dunes.

Anyone who sleeps here finds their rest interrupted by a tall, lanky figure in a white cloak known as [[The Dune Walker]].
