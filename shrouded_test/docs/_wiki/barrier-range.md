The **Barrier Range**, which begins near the Keening Sea and extends north to near the **Kingswood**,** **serves to divide the **City of Shuttered Windows **and the **Duchy of Thring**, especially due to the presence of the Witch Clans. The copper-haired Witchmen are a dangerous breed for arcane power runs through their blood and when it breeds true the children of the hill people are able to manifest the power of their clan's arcane charm. Some are able to spray fire from their fingers, other weave illusions, emit stinking clouds, crawl up ropes to lands that aren’t there, summon demons or lay down geases

The mountains themselves once loomed high but have been worn down by countless eons of wind and rain so that only their eroded remnants remain. But those remnants are still large enough to keep most civilized men away so that the monks of the Temple Invisible and the hill men of the Witch Clans are generally left undisturbed by lowlanders.

Some worry that the Witch Queen or a scion of the old Maddlow kings will be able to unite the clans and bring them down on civilized lands, but for now they squabble incessantly with each other.
## Hex Summary
* Lair of Ushcka and Her Family ([21.15](/wiki/21-15/)): an ogress’s inability to have children has driven her insane.
* The Cave of Sinda the Cursed ([22.15](/wiki/22-15/)): another ogress is cursed to assume the form of a human woman each day.
* The Witch-Hounds ([22.16](/wiki/22-16/)): the lair of strange hounds shaped of mud and straw to serve the Witch Queen.
* Haunt of the Witch Queen ([23.16](/wiki/23-16/)): there are many rumors about her but all agree that that she can return the dead to a sort of life in her cauldron.
* The Land That Wasn't There ([23.18](/wiki/23-18/)): a land full of mad illusions.
* The Ruins of Maddlow Castle ([23.19](/wiki/23-19/)): the remains of a castle built on geases and mad paranoia.
* The Ashberry Mountains ([24.18](/wiki/24-18/)): the Ossorys are famous for their fiery fingers and their fiery wines.
* The Hidden Village of the Wrannows ([24.20](/wiki/24-20/)): an extra dimensional space built on the confluence of a huge number of rope tricks.
* The Gnomes of Mazy Hollow ([25.14](/wiki/25-14/)): the descendants of Gertja Traitor’s-Daughter keep the peace between feuding gods.
* The Monastery of the Temple Invisible ([25.15](/wiki/25-15/)): the mountain monastery of the assassin-monks of the **City of Shuttered Windows**.
* Dragon Lake ([25.16](/wiki/25-16/)): the mountain Witch Clans dive for peacockatrice eggs here.
* The Village of Clan Dunger ([25.17](/wiki/25-17/)): as unpleasant as you’d expect the home of a clan in which every member can cast stinking cloud at will to be.
* The Daemons of the Faustys ([26.16](/wiki/26-16/)): each can summon a different monster.
* The Chequered Room ([26.17](/wiki/26-17/)): great pieces are engaged in what appears to be an ongoing chess game.
