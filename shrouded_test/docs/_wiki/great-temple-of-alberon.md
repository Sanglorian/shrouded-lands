In some dark vault beneath the Great Temple of Alberon, there is stored the Book of Heresies, in which all the heresies from the [Time of Schisms](/wiki/time-of-schisms/) are recorded.

# Connections
* There is a [Central Cathedral](/wiki/central-cathedral/) in the City. It is not clear what the relationship between these two buildings is, or whether they are the same building.
