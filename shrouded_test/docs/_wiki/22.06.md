## The Western Edge of the Kingswood
[[File:2206.png|thumb|400px]]Hex 22.06

Connects to: 13.03

The elves of the Timberlode (13.03) have had the worst possible punishment of the Seelie Court meted out against them: exile from the Kingswood.

Here lies the western edge of the Kingswood and within these borders the elves hold stay and do not treat intruders with kindness. However, the elves stay within their Holt at night and generally only emerge during the day, which makes trespassing into the wood at night fairly common.
