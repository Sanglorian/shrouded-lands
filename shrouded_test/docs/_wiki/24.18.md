## The Ashberry Mountains
Region: [[The Barrier Range]]

<p class="western" lang="en-US" style="margin-bottom:0.14in">Connects to: [05.04](/wiki/05-04/), [20.00](/wiki/20-00/), [23.11](/wiki/23-11/), [25.17](/wiki/25-17/), [26.13](/wiki/26-13/), [26.20](/wiki/26-20/), [29.13](/wiki/29-13/), [29.14.32](/wiki/29-14/), [29.14.35](/wiki/29-14/) and the **Freeholds**.</p>

<p class="western" lang="en-US" style="margin-bottom:0.14in">[[The Osseries]], who shoot jets of flame from their fingertips at will, dwell in this stretch of the **Barrier Peaks**. **Ashberry bushes **are cultivated here by flame from the Osserys; the Osserys use the ashberries to make **Ossery Fire**.</p>
