*The Market Pit is lit by candles made of human finger bones ([28.04](/wiki/28-04/))
*The mountain shepherds of Mt Scorshia eat human finger stew ([02.04](/wiki/02-04/))
*Lady Naideen is missing a finger ([17.11](/wiki/17-11/))
*The araneas of the Gloompatch flee men with a finger removed ([23.08](/wiki/23-08/))
*The Ferryman collects finger bone tolls ([35.06](/wiki/35-06/))
*[Cloud halflings](/wiki/cloud-halflings/) increasingly have six fingers or toes
*Master Var has made a ship in a bottle out of fingernails ([43.03](/wiki/43-03/))
*Ships from the Isle of Thalanyl are covered with human fingernails ([Jahur](/wiki/jahur/))
*[Droesh](/wiki/droesh/) keeps his lover's fingerbone around his neck.
*There was a [War of the Six-Fingered Hand](/wiki/war-of-the-six-fingered-hand/)
*Those who wear the ring of the [Honorable Society of Engineers](/wiki/honorable-society-of-engineers/) without being a recognised member have their middle finger cut off.
