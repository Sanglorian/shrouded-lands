[[Sosaria]]n butchers - along with jerky makers, tanners, cleaners and [[plague doctors]] - are employed by the [[Trunk]] and by the city's [[cattle barons (Sosaria)|cattle barons]] as assassins, demogogues and agitators. 

The original [[Lords Sanguine]] were twelve butchers. Their name comes from their bloody work, and was a pejorative, although they now harvest blood from the Tarrasque which may have encouraged the name to stick. 

The [[butcher birds|butcher bird]] is named after this profession.
