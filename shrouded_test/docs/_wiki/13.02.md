Here, in the foothills of the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/grey-mountains/), is a peaceful glade in which a perfect ring of tall and stately birches grows. This is where the elf [Cerelaine](/wiki/cerelaine/) retreats to when he tires of the people of the Timberlode ([13.03](/wiki/13-03/)). It is also where he keeps his book of spells and several trinkets under the eyes of watchful water elementals.
