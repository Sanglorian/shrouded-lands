## The Glade of Cerelaine
![](/media/1302.png)Connects to: 13.03, 17.07, 22.11, 29.07 and 37.01.

Here, in the foothills of the [[Grey Mountains]], is a peaceful glade in which a perfect ring of tall and stately birches grows. This is where the elf [[Cerelaine]] retreats to when he tires of the people of the Timberlode ([[13.03]]). It is also where he keeps his book of spells and several trinkets under the eyes of watchful [[water elementals]].
