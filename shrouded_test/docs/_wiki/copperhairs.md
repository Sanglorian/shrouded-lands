* *Copperhairs** and **Copperheads** are terms for red-headed people.

Elves once stole copperhair children along the Orphans' March ([27.09](/wiki/27-09/)).

## Connection
* The son of [Lady Natala](/wiki/lady-natala/) - and, potentially, the [Jester Prince](/wiki/jester-prince/) - has copper hair, perhaps inherited from his father.
