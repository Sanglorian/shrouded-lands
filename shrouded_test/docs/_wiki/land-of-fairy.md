Also known as the **Fey-Realm**.
## Features
*There are paths in the Kingswood that lead through the Land of Fairy; the [[Bound Bargainers]] dwell in these passages and truck with fey.
*[[Fey]] live here
*The Orphans' Road might be one of the paths that pass through the Fey-Realm
*The many doors of the Holt ([[29.07]]) might be one and the same with the paths through the Fey-Realm, or they might be another means of egress that also uses the Fey-Realm. The former seems most likely.
*[[Raiders]] will avoid the [[Loyal]] by using pre-arranged passages through the Fey-Realm.
