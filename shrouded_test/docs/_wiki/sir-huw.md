![JesicaLR (CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported)](/media/Aragorn.png)After failing to best a lion in the trial to become a [Brother of the Lion](/wiki/brothers-of-the-lion/), Huw was condemned to a life among a lion pride that wanders [The Devil's Fingers](/wiki/the-devil's-fingers/) and [The Singing Wastes](/wiki/the-singing-wastes/).

Last year, in the pride's migration, he discovered the Cactogre's Canyon ([18.28](/wiki/18-28/)), where the Cactogre lived with his nymph lover. Huw romanced the nymph, and within a week, they eloped.

The nymph began insisting they settle somewhere. They live by a spring-fed pool at the foot of a nameless mountain ([13.24](/wiki/13-24/)). Huw spends his days hunting mountain goats and antelope with stone-tipped spears while his lover tends a pomegranate orchard. She enchants the pomegranates to grant various boons, including healing wounds and improving the body and mind.

However, they rightly suspect that trouble is brewing.
