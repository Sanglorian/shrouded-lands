[Duke Ulthar the Loved](/wiki/ducal-family-of-thring/) and his son [Broderick](/wiki/broderick/) are buried here.

Deep within the tomb, their sword Caledbrand lies and it is said that in **Thring's** hour of greatest need a hero will bring it forth from the tomb and deliver the **Duchy** from its enemies. Many have tried only to die be killed by one of the Sepulchure of the Sword's many traps or killed by the sword itself which strikes with all of the strength of its old master.
