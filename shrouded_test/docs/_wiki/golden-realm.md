The people of the Golden Realm, as do many in the south, have dark skin. It is one of the [Twelve Nations](/wiki/twelve-nations/).

## Connections
* [Cataphracts](/wiki/cataphracts/) are elite troops of the Golden Realm.
* The lady of Adherion said that her slippers were made of silk from the Golden Realm ([30.03](/wiki/30-03/))

* The Golden Realm was recently defeated by an alliance of the gnolls of the Burning Land and the orcs of Grumluda at the [Battle of the Last River](/wiki/battle-of-the-last-river/)

* [Elhanen](/wiki/elhanen/) the Silent has brought cataphracts and blind lion priests to the Shuttered City. She stole [Last Light](/wiki/last-light/), the sword of the [King in Yellow](/wiki/king-in-yellow/).
* The Golden Realm is a centre of worship for the [King in Splendour](/wiki/king-in-splendor/)

* Teodo, the Golden Realm's most sacred artifact, was cast from melted-down swords that were themselves forged from meteors. It was recently taken by the gnolls, but the Great Mother refuses to have it in the City of Smoke ([50.30](/wiki/50-30/)).
* There are Golden Realm slaves in the Burning Lands. One, [Abassi](/wiki/abassi/), has converted "praying gnolls" to the King.
* When the elf court split and the [unseelie elves](/wiki/unseelie-elves/) departed, it is said that they went - along with the [horses](/wiki/horses/) - by white barge to the Golden Realm. Also among the Twelve Nations is [Alacoda](/wiki/alacoda/), nation of the high elves.
* Zealots who were expelled from the Golden Realm for their extreme views live in a tower ([07.33](/wiki/07-33/)), fight with gleaming metal armour, are blind except for their one-eyed leader, and torture people to convert them to the worship of the [King in Splendour](/wiki/king-in-splendor/).
