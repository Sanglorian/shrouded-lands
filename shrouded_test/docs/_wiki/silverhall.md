Silverhall

Located in [31.04](/wiki/31-04/)

Within Olmsted keep is a great hall in which the entire back wall is plated with silver. This silver is chaped in intricate designs of strange whurls and gemometrical patterns.

In front of the silver wall is a raised dais on which is placed a small round table at which Lord Olmsted and those favored by him sit to feast.

Those Lord Olmsted has called to his side have noted that in the presence of the silver wall a powerful sense of wellbeing comes over from them and they seem to recover from hurts and sickness at a pace that is far faster than normal. More importantly, the silverwall seems to render those who drink before it completely impervious to hangovers.
