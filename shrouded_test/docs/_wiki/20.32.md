## Abyss of Prism Crabs
Region: the Singing Wastes

Connects to: [19.31](/wiki/19-31/), [19.31.07](/wiki/19-31/) and [41.24](/wiki/41-24/).
## Overview
The crabs that live exclusively in this black sinkhole several miles offshore from [Jahur](/wiki/jahur/) have [Abyss Diamonds](/wiki/abyss-diamonds/) on their backs.

Sadly, few who challenge the abyss return. Survivors tell of water pressure that crushes bones, and of horrific predators with blazing eyes, glowing tendrils and teeth like falchions. [Kwalish](/wiki/kwalish/) met a [water weird](/wiki/water-weirds/) in the deeps, possibly here. 
## Hooks
* Do any predators of the abyss seek prey closer to the surface?
