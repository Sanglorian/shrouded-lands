## Abyss of Prism Crabs
Region: the Singing Wastes

Connects to: 19.31, 19.31.07 and 41.24.
## Overview
The crabs that live exclusively in this black sinkhole several miles offshore from [[Jahur]] have [[Abyss Diamonds]] on their backs.

Sadly, few who challenge the abyss return. Survivors tell of water pressure that crushes bones, and of horrific predators with blazing eyes, glowing tendrils and teeth like falchions. [[Kwalish]] met a [[water weirds|water weird]] in the deeps, possibly here. 
## Hooks
*Do any predators of the abyss seek prey closer to the surface?
