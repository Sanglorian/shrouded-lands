A battle between [Blind Midshotgatepool](/wiki/blind-midshotgatepool/) and the [Shuttered City](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/), after which Shuttered asserted its control over Blind Midshotgatepool.

[Death's Lovelies](/wiki/death's-lovelies/) formed during this battle, when they realised that the promise of a cure was a false one.
