## Overview
This heavily forested and mountainous area is home to a powerful sapient bear, one of the mighty [nordanbjorn](/wiki/nordanbjorn/). This bjornajarl, [Thorek Ironhide](/wiki/thorek-ironhide/), dwells in a cave halfway up the mountain with his sleuth of lesser bears.

The bjornajarl does not spend much time in his cave, for he is usually roaming [the lands of his marche](/wiki/the-marche-of-the-winterjarl/).
