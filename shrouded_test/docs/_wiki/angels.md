* The [Destroying Angel](/wiki/destroying-angel/) is the only occupant of a cave system through which the Glass Rapids run ([07.06](/wiki/07-06/)).
* The [Dune Walker](/wiki/dune-walker/) may be demon, angel or fey, or a restless spirit.
* A regular of [The Mud Platter](/wiki/the-mud-platter/) has mistaken [Armand of the Axe](/wiki/armand-of-the-axe/) for an angel of [Alberon](/wiki/alberon/).
* The Shepherd, a giant shepherd with a frozen beard, burning white eyes and a golden cudgel has apparently rescued mountaineers on Mount Scorshia ([02.03](/wiki/02-03/)). He may be a frost giant, a hill giant, an angel or a figment of the imagination.
* The [Sealed Library](/wiki/sealed-library/) contains the *Encyclopædia Angelos*, a gnostic encyclopedia detailing the True Names, pictures, sigils, and general description of every angel known to man, and instructions on how to summon and bind them.
* The [Treatise on Salt](/wiki/treatise-on-salt/) claims that salt was once the purest substance in the Shrouded Lands, foremd when angels fell from the sky during a forgotten war. Upon hitting the ground, their bodies turned to salt and shattered. The salt could neutralise poison or halt aging, but absorbed the world's impurities and now is only potent enough to preserve food and ward off minor demons.
