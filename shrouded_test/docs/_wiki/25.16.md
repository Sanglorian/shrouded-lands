[25.16](/wiki/25-16/)

* *Region: **[The Barrier Range](/wiki/the-barrier-range/)

Connects to: *[30.12](/wiki/30-12/)* and *[37.21](/wiki/37-21/)*.

This dragon-shaped lake is where men and women of the surrounding Witch Clans dive for [peacockatrice eggs](/wiki/peacockatrices/). They are generally found in the coldest and deepest reaches of the lake, which makes them hard to retrieve, what with all of the electric eels and carnivorous fish.

However, the high prices these eggs can fetch in **Shuttered** can make the diving worth it. Legend says that one day a dragon egg will be discovered here which makes the lake all the more intriguing
#### Hooks
* Why are there peacockatrice eggs at the bottom of a lake?
* What kind of fish?
* Is there really a connection between this lake and dragons?
