## Dragon Lake
Connects to: *[30.12](/wiki/30-12/)* and *[37.21](/wiki/37-21/)*.

<p class="western" lang="en-US" style="margin-bottom:0.14in">This dragon-shaped lake is where men and women of the surrounding Witch Clans dive for [peacockatrice eggs](/wiki/peacockatrices/). They are generally found in the coldest and deepest reaches of the lake, which makes them hard to retrieve, what with all of the electric eels and carnivorous fish.</p>

<p class="western" lang="en-US" style="margin-bottom:0.14in">However, the high prices these eggs can fetch in **Shuttered** can make the diving worth it. Legend says that one day a dragon egg will be discovered here which makes the lake all the more intriguing</p>
