The lands surrounding the Citadel of the Verlime ([18.07](/wiki/18-07/)) were known far and wide for their mild climate and fertile soil, and the [House of Verlime](/wiki/verlimes/) built its doomed fortress on the proceeds of the taxes on the farmers who worked those lands. The wine, [pipe-weed](/wiki/pipe-weed/), and distinctive purple honey produced there were famous as far away as the [Bitter Coast](/wiki/bitter-coast/), and the region provided much of the grain, pork and [ostrich meat](/wiki/ostriches/) necessary to feed the south. When the Citadel of Verlime fell to the [elves](/wiki/elves/), many fled for fear that the elves would soon turn their attentions beyond the ruined fortress, but the sack never came.

The next five years were hard on the farmers, and though the elves left them be, bandits and [orc](/wiki/orcs/) raiders were not so kind. Food shortages lead to widespread hunger and some venerable vintages vanished forever. Soon, however, the lean times drove humans and [halflings](/wiki/halflings/) from far and wide to working the fallow fields of the Verlimes, either out of greed or out of a simple desire to have enough to eat. The farms no longer were under the protection of any noble house, but the farmers would pay small bands of mercenaries to protect them from the orcs. To this day, many young adventurers get their start defending the Freehold for the customary wage of 15 sp a day, and many old adventurers retire there after a big score.

Today, though, some of the richest families are beginning to consider themselves aristocracy, and are no longer satisfied with mere wealth. Some, like the noble families they ape, have simply decided to call themselves a House, while others, like the [Furhoofs](/wiki/furhoofs/) ([17.05](/wiki/17-05/)), have bought titles from the Duke of Thring, and all five of the [Blackwort brothers](/wiki/blackworts/) have purchased citizens' charters from the [City of Shuttered Windows](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/). Worse, wealthy Freeholders are starting to hire their own men at arms, and have started raising their eldest sons and daughters to be warriors instead of farmers, and those would-be knights threaten to tear the community apart with drunken brawls that escalate into violent family feuds.

### Connections:
* After the end of the [goblins wars](/wiki/goblins-wars/), in which the orcs and dwarves destroyed the goblin forests that once lay between the [Kingswood](/wiki/kingswood/) and the coast ([24.02](/wiki/24-02/)), these lands were ruled by the [High Kings of Gore](/wiki/high-kings-of-gore/) ([05.20](/wiki/05-20/)). The Verlimes were able to carve out their Duchy after the fall of the High Kings.
