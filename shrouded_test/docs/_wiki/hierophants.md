The [[Hierophant of Ninbolm]] governs [[Ninbolm]]

[[Amelar the Immaculate]] is known as the Hierophant of Constant Sorrow.

Those [[Brothers of the Spear]] who defied their Hierophant's order to join the [[Temple Invisible]] were transformed by him or her into the wretches who call themselves the [[Temple of Alberon]]. He or she is presumably the precursor to the [[Unseen Hierophant]].
