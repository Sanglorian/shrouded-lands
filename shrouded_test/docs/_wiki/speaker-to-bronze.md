A [[dwarves|dwarven]] deity. The sister-brother of the [[Drinker of Iron]]. According to legend, when the Drinker of Iron cut off his-her head, the Speaker to Bronze's body sunk into the ground of Titan's Rest ([[33.01]]) and his-her head rolled to rest upon the mountain into which Titan's Skull is built ([[33.00]]).
