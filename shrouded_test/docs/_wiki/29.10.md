## Tehaar's Hunting Grounds
![](/media/2910.png)Hex [29.10](/wiki/29-10/)

Although [[Tehaar the Huntress]] ranges widely across the Kingswood in search of intruders, where she can be found most often is at the far southern border of the Kingswood where the forest approaches the City of Shuttered Windows. Here is where humanity swarms the thickest and where it must be watched the most closely.
