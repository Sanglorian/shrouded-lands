Gnomes are one of the peoples of the Shrouded Lands.![A gnome gathering.](/media/JohnBauer12.jpg)

## Ninbolm
[[Ninbolm]] is a gnomish city carved into a giant petrified stump. It is ruled by the [[Hierophant]], who merged with the stump, and hosts the subversive illusionist school, the [[Indigo Academy]]. 

Gnomes from Ninbolm built the Glass Eye ([[01.23]]). Five gnomes from Ninbolm accompany Iocannus, the astrologer hoping to reverse-engineer the Eye.

One of the twin towns of [[Olgam and Oskill]] was founded by migrants from Ninbolm fleeing the Hierophant's "tyranny". Both towns were destroyed. 

## Dust Walkers
[[Dust Walkers]] are secretive gnomes in [[The Devil's Fingers]] who make significant use of sandalwood - including making sandals out of it. Bare toes are a good sign that a person is not an illusion, as getting toes just right is tricky for illusionists. 

## Giants' Mortar/Gnomes and the Shuttered City
Giants' Mortar is a term for constructions that include gnomes living within the building. This makes the construction seem miraculous, since it is always kept excellently repaired.

[[Gnome ghosts]] inhabit the walls of [[Shuttered]], cursed by deep dwarves to build up the walls that they let fall at a crucial moment. 

Gnomes serving as Giants' Mortar also let [[Loguiso's Wall]] fall, although that was because Shuttered reneged on their promised payment. 

There is a Gnomish Quarter in Shuttered; one of its key features is the [[Temple of the Labyrinth]] - of whom a gnome is the High Priestess. 

## Other
* [[The Giant and the Gnome]] is a reference to a gnome pirate (possibly legendary) who fell in love with a stone giant. 
* The [[deep dwarves]] hate gnomes and gouge their eyes out, due to their grudge related to the gnomish betrayal of the [[Shuttered City]].
