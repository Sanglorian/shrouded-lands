[[Stone giants]] developed the Stern Way, a philosophy and martial art. They will teach it to others. It focuses on throws and blocks.
==Connections==
*The Enigma, an enormous stone that resembles a bald head, is a site of pilgrimage for adherents, but details of it are absent from their teachings ([[07.08]])
*Master Memin is a stone giant who teaches the Stern Way in his Sunken Cathedral ([[13.20]]). One manoeuvre, the Emerald Priest Lunge, is known only by him.
*Monks of the Stern Way disagree with the meaning of the Long Table, saying that it is the tomb of a dead stone giant emperor who once ruled the world ([[06.03]])
