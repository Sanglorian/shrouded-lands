==Description==
To use this template, copy the following code and fill in the appropriate fields.

==Syntax==
<pre>{{infobox episode
 | title       = 
 | season      = 
 | number      = 
 | image       = [e.g. "Example.jpg"]
 | imagewidth  = [e.g. "150"] [default: 210 pixels]
 | airdate     = 
 | writer      = 
 | director    = 
 | previous    = 
 | next        = 
}}</pre>

==Sample output==
<pre>{{infobox episode
 | title       = Flower of Babel
 | season      = One
 | number      = Three
 | image       = Example.jpg
 | airdate     = Unaired
 | writer      = D. Righta
 | director    = A. di Rektour
 | previous    = Episode Two
 | next        = Episode Four
}}</pre>

Results in...
