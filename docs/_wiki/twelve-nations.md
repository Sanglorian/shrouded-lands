The [Shuttered City](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/) embassies for three of these nations have been bricked up. One of those is Alacoda's; the other two have 'fallen to barbarism'. Naros' embassy is bricked up; it's unclear if it's counted among the two.
* [Alacoda](/wiki/alacoda/), empire of the [high elves](/wiki/high-elves/) and favoured of the [Green Lady](/wiki/green-lady/)
* [Golden Realm](/wiki/golden-realm/) (pays tribute to the [gnolls](/wiki/gnolls/) of the [Burning Lands](/wiki/the-burning-lands/))
* [Grumluda](/wiki/grumluda/), domain of the Eternal-King-of-All-Orcs and importer of ferocious beasts
* [Naros](/wiki/naros/) (fallen)
* [Minotaur nation] (conquered by orcs) ([18.02](/wiki/18-02/))

## Connections
* [Embassies for at least some of these nations](/wiki/embassies-(shuttered)/) exist in [Shuttered](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/).
