![](/media/Enigma1.png)**Inspired by:** 'All I Needed to Know, I Learned from D&D' in *Dragon 288*.
Connects to: [06.03](/wiki/06-03/), [18.10.01](/wiki/18-10/) and [19.01](/wiki/19-01/).

One of the [witchways](/wiki/witchways/) or [merlinburrows](/wiki/merlinburrows/) ([19.01](/wiki/19-01/)) that leads from the Sunless Sea ([18.10.01](/wiki/18-10/)) forks about a mile below the surface. The lefthand path leads to an open cavern, the center of which is occupied by a basalt slab at least fifty yards high. Strange words are carved around the base of the slab, and people who consider what the stone would look like from a distance realise that it would resemble an enormous bald head.

The head is a site of pilgrimage for adherents of the [Stern Way](/wiki/stern-way/) ([06.03](/wiki/06-03/)), but they claim that the head is absent from their myths and histories.
