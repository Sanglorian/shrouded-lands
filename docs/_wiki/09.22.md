Southwest of the borders of Thring there lies a broad lake where cormorants nest in the shallows. Further out into the water, there are schools of enormous fish with the ability to swim through the air as easily as the water. Though they possess this remarkable power, they seem uninterested in using it, and will only fly up out of the lake if they are threatened by an attack from below. Because of this, the [flying fish](/wiki/flying-fish/) were known as the 'fat fish' until six months ago when their secret was discovered by the huntsman Lorenzo DiMarr.

An expedition from the Shuttered City captured twelve flying fish and brought them to the Shuttered City ([read more](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/))
