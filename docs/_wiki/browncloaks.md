The Browncloaks include at least [Elwen](/wiki/elwen/) the [Snickersnee](/wiki/snickersnees/) and her companions, a one-legged [gnomish illusionist](/wiki/gnomes/), a [crowfolk](/wiki/crowfolk/) ratter and a masked [deep dwarven](/wiki/deep-dwarves/) machinist. They are drinking in Farnsfall ([11.03](/wiki/11-03/)) and claim to have looted a hidden [dragon](/wiki/dragons/) cave - leaving behind mountains of copper pennies ([08.01](/wiki/08-01/)).
