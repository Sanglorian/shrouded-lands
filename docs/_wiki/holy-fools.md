A heretical sect of [Iano](/wiki/iano/) that stirs dissent among the lower classes.

The Barking Dogs are Holy Fools tasked with spreading hidden information.

A bad batch of [bucket kelp](/wiki/bucket-kelp/) that induces terrifying hallucinations has been blamed on the Holy Fools (and others).

The [Idjits](/wiki/idjits/) suspect the Holy Fools of sabotaging the [tempest](/wiki/tempests/) of the Tower of Idjit ([19.30](/wiki/19-30/)) so it produces [tempest spawn](/wiki/tempest-spawn/).

Holy Fools take advantage of the dead ends and irrational turns of the [Alleys of the Woman in Red](/wiki/alleys-of-the-woman-in-red/). They use isolated courtyards as open-air meeting places.
## Holy Fools
* Some Viceroys suspect [Isane the Beauty](/wiki/isane-the-beauty/) of being a Holy Fool.
* [Lunfar](/wiki/lunfar/) the grocer and illicit mapseller is a Barking Dog.
