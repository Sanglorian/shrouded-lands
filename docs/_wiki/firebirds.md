### Connections
* Firebirds cavort at the Pool of the Firebirds ([04.05](/wiki/04-05/))
* The dragon cultists of Cragsend ([04.06](/wiki/04-06/)) believe that ritually sacrificing firebirds will help to awaken the legendary phoenix, which plays an important role in their beliefs.
* Lady Naideen has a firebird bought for her by her father. She can enlarge it and use it as a mount ([17.11](/wiki/17-11/)).
* It seems that the firebirds mate in the Badlands Where the Beetles Dwell ([06.05](/wiki/06-05/)).
* One young lady dreamed of a firebird and woke up with a feather of one in her hand ([39.23.01](/wiki/39-23/)).
* One of the wives of the Doge himself wears fresh-plucked firebird feathers on her dresses ([29.14.08](/wiki/29-14/)).
