![](/media/1302.png)Connects to: [13.03](/wiki/13-03/), [17.07](/wiki/17-07/), [22.11](/wiki/22-11/), [29.07](/wiki/29-07/) and [37.01](/wiki/37-01/).

Here, in the foothills of the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/grey-mountains/), is a peaceful glade in which a perfect ring of tall and stately birches grows. This is where the elf [Cerelaine](/wiki/cerelaine/) retreats to when he tires of the people of the Timberlode ([13.03](/wiki/13-03/)). It is also where he keeps his book of spells and several trinkets under the eyes of watchful [water elementals](/wiki/water-elementals/).
