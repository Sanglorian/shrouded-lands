Rumors persist that [Tuma Yedaard](/wiki/tuma-yedaard/) himself is the legendary Huu Vo Malkus, a mysterious Raider ([factions - elves](/wiki/factions-elves/)) who single-handily makes a big score once every decade or so, donating the entirety of his catch to the [Weeper](/wiki/the-weeper/) for distribution among the Simple.
## Connection:
['Maud' Verlime](/wiki/maud-verlime/) claimes to have chased off Huu Vo Malkus from her grazing lands ([17.07](/wiki/17-07/)).
