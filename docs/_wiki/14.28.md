*Inspired by:* Vapes, Ninth World Bestiary

One of the monstrosities that has crawled forth from the [Breath of the Earth](/wiki/breath-of-the-earth/) is a strange wolfen predator.

Called the fresco beast by gnolls because from one angle it looks like a long wolf painted on a wall, the alien is flat. Turned to face its prey, it disappears – except for its toothy jaws.

The [Nekh](/wiki/nekh/) are particularly distressed by its appearance in their territory, and will pay handsomely for it to be somehow disposed of. Their concern may be heightened by a “flattening” of the land around the beast's lair, as rocks thin and sharpen, the clouds turn blocky and the cactuses contract into mere sketches.

### Hooks
* Where is the fresco beast from? Are there others like it?
* How do you slay somoething that only exists in two dimensions?
* What does it think about this more substantial world?
* How does it eat? What does it eat?
