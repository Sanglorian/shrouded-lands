Also known as the **Lord of Pain**.
![Alberon strikes off one of Tiamat's heads.](/media/8775854218_c0815c0cb4_b.jpg)
## Features
* The [Hundred Heresies](/wiki/hundred-heresies/) are dictated by the [Temple Indivisible](/wiki/temple-indivisible/)
* The [Temple Invisible](/wiki/temple-invisible/) no longer follow Alberon
* The [Temple Indivisible](/wiki/temple-indivisible/) is the main church of Alberon
* The [Canes Sanguis](/wiki/canes-sanguis/) is the most powerful of the Priests-Militant of Alberon
* The [Reluctant Temple of Alberon](/wiki/reluctant-temple-of-alberon/) claims that Alberon would conquer the world but he is trapped by his worshippers within the City walls
* The [Time of Schisms](/wiki/time-of-schisms/) was a period where the priests of Alberon divided and then united
* Alberon appears in one of [eleven recognised forms](/wiki/eleven-recognised-forms/)

## Places of worship
* The Farthest Hermitage ([15.00](/wiki/15-00/)) is the most distant outpost of Alberon's worshippers
