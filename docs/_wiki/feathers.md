* [Quicklings](/wiki/quicklings/) dress in feathers.
* The [Sorcerer of Tarengael Town](/wiki/sorcerer-of-tarengael-town/) specialises in featherstitch magic.
* The Sparrow Knight has a feather token which lets him float. He also claims that he knows the origin of [Sir Waine and Tycell](/wiki/sir-waine-and-tycell/)'s feather token, which also let them float.
* The traveller's journal (see [Pirate Kings](/wiki/pirate-kings/)) begins with him clutching a white feather.
* The actress who was to play Ulena in [The Song of Ban and Ulena](/wiki/song-of-ban-and-ulena/) awoke clutching a glowing feather.
* The people of Everdark ([01.03](/wiki/01-03/)) wear clothes woven from the long vanes of the feathers of great rocs. The clothes are waterproof, warm and comfortable.
* [Geddar](/wiki/geddar/) of the [Goblin Markets](/wiki/goblin-markets/) offers all of her wealth to any who can bring her a tail feather of [the mockingbird](/wiki/the-mockingbird/).
* Lizardfolk trucked with beings of vine and feather.
* Dinosaur feathers are a [ritual currency](/wiki/ritual-currency/).
* A pillow stuffed with [cat feathers](/wiki/cat-feathers/) puts those who are muffled by it to sleep.
* [Peacockatrices](/wiki/peacockatrices/)' feathers dazzle the mind of those who look at them.
* [Firebirds](/wiki/firebirds/)' feathers give off a warm glow.
* [Red-Feathers](/wiki/red-feathers/) is a crowfolk outlaw.
* [Asrigh](/wiki/asrigh/) forbids keeping feathered creatures in bondage.
* Doves on the Long Stairs ([03.05](/wiki/03-05/)) have their feathers died orange in honour of the Drinker of Iron.
* The ladies of [Shuttered](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/) wear feathers.
* A feathered hairstyle among the dandies of [Shuttered](/wiki/city-of-shuttered-windows/) was inspired by [Simone the Fowl](/wiki/simone-the-fowl/).
* [Eliana](/wiki/eliana/) inspired the residents of Shuttered to wear feathers, though few could match her firebird feather dresses, plucked so freshly that they still glowed.
* [Painted elephants](/wiki/painted-elephants/) have brightly-coloured feathers and fur.
* The pointing bones used by the [Howling College](/wiki/howling-college/) are wrapped in feathers.
