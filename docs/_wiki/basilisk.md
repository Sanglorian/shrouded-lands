The Basilisk dwells at the top of the Pilgrim's Spire ([25.31](/wiki/25-31/)), turning mendicants to stone.

Seven dancers of the [Shadow Ballet](/wiki/shadow-ballet/) in the [Grey Comedy](/wiki/grey-comedy/) are allegedly [firebirds](/wiki/firebirds/) turned to stone by the Basilisk and then revived by [Rosetta](/wiki/rosetta/).

The author of the [Unifying Theory of Blood](/wiki/unifying-theory-of-blood/) speculates that the Basilisk or [Medusa](/wiki/medusa/) may be infected by elemental earth, and would turn to air in the sunlight.
