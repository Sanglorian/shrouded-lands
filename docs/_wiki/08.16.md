The Eyrie is the only nest of [The Scarecrows](/wiki/scarecrows/) that is publicly known. It is a watchtower, abandoned after the collapse of the Kingdom of Gore. At any time, some two dozen or more Scarecrows and twice as many guests, diplomats, lovers, children and other civilians are present.
