## Connections
[Dryads](/wiki/dryads/) killed by [vampires](/wiki/vampires/) become corpse trees ([11.11](/wiki/11-11/)).

Unique among the dryads, hawthorn dryads can chop down their shrub and fashion it into a still-living but functional item.

Supendar, a dryad planted before the fall of Bergolast, is responsible for the presence of the dryads in the Shrouded Lands - and for the fall of [Naros](/wiki/naros/).
* [Count Seutorian](/wiki/count-seutorian/)'s parties include transplanted dryads.

## Dryads
* [Laughing Sam](/wiki/laughing-sam/) is a dryad from Naros, but has forgotten his past. His tree is a willow on the Bitter Coast.
* The woman who ended the [Seelie-Unseelie wars](/wiki/seelie-unseelie-wars/) was a hawthorn dryad

* A forest dell in Gore ([051.14](/wiki/05114/)) is home to the largest grove of [dryads](/wiki/dryads/) on this side of the Keening Sea. Rather than growing here naturally, all of the trees of the dryads were transplanted here fifteen years ago when [Naros](/wiki/naros/) fell to the curse of the arch-lich Supendar ([29.14.12](/wiki/29-14/)).
* The near-death of her tree makes a dryad in the Kingswood ravenous ([32.05](/wiki/32-05/))

* The sequoia dryad who tended Broderick's Grove ([02.07](/wiki/02-07/)) gave up her tree's heartwood to her druid lover, but now [Udenyr](/wiki/udenyr/) holds it and uses it to command her
