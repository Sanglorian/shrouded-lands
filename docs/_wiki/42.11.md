Baelton is a sprawling, walled village home to several hundred "[tieflings](/wiki/tieflings/)". It is the largest of [Tief](/wiki/tief/)'s several holdings, where he keeps his tieflings. Baelton's gates are open to outsiders, but few non-tieflings wish to go here because of the disturbing appearance and powers of the tieflings.
