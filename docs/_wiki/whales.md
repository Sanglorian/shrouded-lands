> **Leviathans** redirects here. For the leviathans of the earth, see [46.09](/wiki/46-09/).
Also called **leviathans**.
* [Old Leviathan](/wiki/old-leviathan/) is a figure in the faith of the [King in Splendour](/wiki/king-in-splendor/).
* A sailor outside the Cave of Kull Cove claims to have hunted whales on the [Ocean of Bitter Regrets](/wiki/ocean-of-bitter-regrets/) and sells rancid whalebits. The squid within is terrified of whales and the smell of whales.
* The song of the whales of the Ocean of Bitter Regrets sustains the [Dreamsong](/wiki/dreamsong/), and whale foetuses listening to the song learn secrets before they are even born.
* [Lady Belal](/wiki/lady-belal/) wants to weave a net so vast it can snare a whale.
* Rogdag hunts bearded whales, among other things. [35.13](/wiki/35-13/)

* A white whale is trapped in the [Sunless Sea](/wiki/sunless-sea/), and its agonised cries tarnish the Dreamsong.
* The druids of the Coast ([02.07](/wiki/02-07/)) cut gigantic didgeridoos that can summon whales from the depths.
