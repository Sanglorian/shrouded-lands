Very few places in the Shrouded Lands lie beyond the City of Shuttered Windows’ grasp. But the City cannot always win. When Melnir’s Mount rose, it destroyed one of Shuttered’s most prosperous colonies. The calamity’s survivors turned their back on the City and began worshipping an enormous crayfish. The mindless abomination grows with every feeding, and it now rivals the Bitter Sea’s greatest whales. Even more dangers lurk in this perilous backwater. The man-eating creature known as the Heget lives on an isle near the old colony, awaiting wayward sailors with her brood of ravenous sons.
