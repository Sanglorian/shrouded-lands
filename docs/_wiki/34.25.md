Razorgrass is one of the most common forms of vegetation to be found in the Burning Lands. It is an aggressive weed and, although it burns at least once a year, it regrows at an astonishing pace and often reaches heights feet taller than a man’s head. It is well named, for its edges are surprisingly sharp, making it dangerous to pass through, especially when the hot wings blow out of the south.

In this particular area, the razorgrass grows thicker than anywhere else in the Burning Lands and often seems to twitch and whip, even though when the air is still. Local gnolls enjoy chasing intruders into the grass and giggle raucously as the grass slices away any exposed flesh.
