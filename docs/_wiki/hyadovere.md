[Hyfalls](/wiki/hyfalls/) ([40.20](/wiki/40-20/)) was built on the ruins of Hyadovere, a colony of [Bergolast](/wiki/bergolast/). It was presumably named for the [River Hyad](/wiki/river-hyad/).
