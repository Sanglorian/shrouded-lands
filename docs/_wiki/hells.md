![](/media/Demonic.jpg)A [Hell-born spirit](/wiki/hell-born-spirits/) infects the [Mirror Throne](/wiki/mirror-throne/) ([16.16](/wiki/16-16/)); it was summoned when the throne was used as a [sorcerous foci](/wiki/sorcerers/).

When the [Bell of Six Answers](/wiki/bell-of-six-answers/) in Castle Brucoloc ([08.21](/wiki/08-21/)) is tolled, it will apparently summon an Imp from Hell. 

May be related to [fiends](/wiki/fiends/).

The [Forest of Abominations](/wiki/forest-of-abominations/) releases dust from a dying universe, including decaying hells.
