## Overview
This hex contains the Stormhead: a perpetual storm that, with the appropriate 'spark', turns a cloud giant into a storm giant (see [The Stormhead](/wiki/creatures/)).

In a cave along these cliffs, a Pirate King has stashed her spark. It was too beautiful for her to relinquish, and so she built a fortress to protect it in readiness for the day when she steels herself to sail to the Stormhead.

There are islands to the west of the Stormhead. Most of the giants who undergo the transformation swim to them before the shore.
